### Added
- ORPHAN FLIGHT: a departed FLIGHT whose AIRCRAFT session died (for example cut by a usage limit) is no longer lost when the same REGISTRATION comes back as a new session that never holds it. atc finds it from the departure record, raises one CAUTION ALERT and one HOME line after a grace period (default 15 min, setting), and offers an English RESUME text (FLIGHT, STAND, pushed WIP commit, uncommitted changes, no new STAND) as a RELAY draft the SUPERVISOR sends with one click; atc and OCC send nothing themselves. DISPATCH now counts the orphan against that REGISTRATION's slot from the first plan, so it gets no other FLIGHT while the orphan is unresolved. A SUPERVISOR-only switch `ORPHAN FLIGHT` (default on) turns detection, alert, HOME line and counting off together; misfires (alerts that cleared by themselves within 15 minutes, holds that were released within the grace period) show in METRICS → MISFIRE ([docs/fleet.md](docs/fleet.md), [docs/dispatch.md](docs/dispatch.md)).
