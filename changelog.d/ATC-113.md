### Changed
- DISPATCH and SCHEDULE list what needs the SUPERVISOR first (ATC-113, [docs/guide/screens.md](docs/guide/screens.md)).
  - DISPATCH order: mode line, exceptions, ASSIGN / HELD / RELEASE proposals, IN FLIGHT, ARRIVED candidates, AIRCRAFT and HOLD_DEPARTURE, RECENT, READINESS. SCHEDULE order: mode line, LATE WAYPOINTS, DRAFTS, IN PROGRESS, CANDIDATES, RECENT, READINESS.
  - READINESS is one collapsed line (`READINESS · 2b 5/8 · gate 12/20 ✗ · agree 92% ✓ · CROSSCHECK 93%`). Expanding it shows the 2b checklist, STAGE 2b, STAGE 3, the ATFM shadow judgements, FLIGHT FOLLOWING and VECTORS · DIRECT (DISPATCH), or STAGE S2 and ROUTES WITHOUT WAYPOINTS (SCHEDULE), unchanged. Open or closed is remembered per tab.
  - Exceptions stay open at the top: an active ATFM GROUND STOP or a failing main CI (with the GROUND STOP switches and ATFM OFF), and FLIGHT FOLLOWING FLIGHTs with a delay or mismatch (up to three, with a button to the full panel). Without an exception, the switches and ATFM OFF are inside READINESS.
  - No block or number was removed, and the brief API and gate rules are unchanged.
