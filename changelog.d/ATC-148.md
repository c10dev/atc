### Added
- ACCOUNT CHANGE (ATC-148, [docs/fleet.md](docs/fleet.md) 8.6, [docs/accounts.md](docs/accounts.md) 5.3): a new FLEET PLAN kind that proposes moving an AIRCRAFT to another registered ACCOUNT when its observed ACCOUNT is at `holdPct` (or has a LIMIT more than `accountChangeLimitMin`, default 60, from its reset), or to come back home once home is below `infoPct`. Only between FLIGHTs (idle background session, no FLIGHT held or kept, no open PR); a live FLIGHT is never moved and a limit cut stays RESUME on the same ACCOUNT. The target is the registered, not logged-out ACCOUNT below `infoPct` and under its cap with the lowest use. On approval atc checks the target, stops the session on the old ACCOUNT and launches it with its CREW BRIEFING on the new one, and writes one `account-change` FLIGHT RECORDER event. Nothing moves by itself and there is no automatic mode.
- FUEL leaks: the new session's cold first request is counted as `ACCOUNT CHANGE` (next to SESSION CHANGE and COLD CACHE). The FLEET card reads `flying on acct-1 (home acct-2)`.

### Changed
- With a target ACCOUNT available, a weekly LIMIT alone no longer proposes AOG for that AIRCRAFT.
- `docs/fleet.md` 8.8 and `docs/fuel.md` 6 no longer say atc never switches accounts; they say what it proposes.
