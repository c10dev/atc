### Changed
- BRAKES moved from the bottom of HOME into the bottom panel as a tab beside CONTROL, so the brakes are one keystroke away on every screen (ATC-455, [docs/layout.md](docs/layout.md) "Z5 as built").
  - The panel header has two tabs, `CONTROL` and `BRAKES` (real tabs: arrow keys move between them, the chosen tab is remembered in this browser). The BRAKES body holds what HOME's BRAKES line held: GROUND STOP and manual-stop counts, `ATFM…`, `STOP ALL…`, the automation switches, the DISPATCH and SCHEDULE mode switches and `스위치 설정`. Nothing opens before it is pressed; the confirmations are unchanged.
  - Folded, the BRAKES tab shows the count with a word when a stop is in force (`BRAKES 1 STOP`), otherwise nothing. The panel header is now always drawn.
  - HOME no longer draws BRAKES (the `AtfmAlert` banner stays), and the sidebar's HOME anchor for it is gone. No new effects.
