### Added
- Header CONTROL strip: one chip per control session (`TWR OCC MCC XCHK REV ENG`) under the tab row, e.g. `MCC ● 5m · 2분 전` (ATC-127, [docs/fleet.md](docs/fleet.md) 8.5.1 "Header CONTROL strip as built").
  - States `ok`, `working`, `needs`, `late` (last tick older than 2 × the `/loop` interval + 1 min) and `down`. The last tick is the SQUELCH time, or the session's last activity when there is no SQUELCH record. The tooltip shows the kind, job detail and `needs`, the interval, the last tick and its source, and QUIET counts.
  - A click opens FLEET › CONTROL SESSIONS. Below 768 px it folds into one `CTRL n/6` chip. Display only: no LAUNCH or STOP, no new alert.
  - `GET /api/control/sessions` now carries `squelch` per session and caches the `claude agents` read for 30 s (`?fresh=1` skips it).
