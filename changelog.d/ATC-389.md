### Added
- FUEL usage comparisons on `#metrics/fuel` (ATC-389):
  - **TODAY** at the top, always shown: today from midnight (the viewer's time zone) against yesterday up to the same time — COST, requests, work time and ARRIVED.
  - FUEL COST and requests in the summary row gain the previous period of the same length and the change.
  - **USAGE** under the summary: work time (5-minute slots with a request, CAPTAIN and CREW), active AIRCRAFT, ARRIVED FLIGHTs, PRs and cost per ARRIVED against the previous period, plus 8 weekly rows.
  - A change is shown only when the compared period is fully covered by transcripts; otherwise the label says how much history there is.
  - New read-only `GET /api/fuel/trend?days=N&tz=M`.
