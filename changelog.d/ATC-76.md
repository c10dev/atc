### Added
- FLEET shows each AIRCRAFT's session origin and permission mode, and STOP, RESTART and REFRESH act only on background sessions (ATC-76, [docs/fleet.md](docs/fleet.md) 8.5.2). On 2026-09-28 all ten interactive team sessions were desktop sessions, and origin decided control, account, lifetime and 2b delivery.
  - Origin: `background` from the session's `kind`; otherwise the process command line and its parent's, read once per pid from `/proc` (read-only, no signal, no attach, no account identity): `~/.claude/remote/…` is `desktop`, a plain `claude` is `terminal`, anything else `unknown`. The permission mode comes from `--permission-mode`, or for background sessions from the LAUNCH record that started them; control LAUNCH records now carry `permissionMode` too.
  - `GET /api/fleet` has `origin` and `permissionMode` on every AIRCRAFT. The FLEET list row and card show `BG`, `DESKTOP`, `TERM` or `?` with the permission mode, replacing `BG <id>` (the id is in the tooltip). Cards of non-background sessions show the manual steps for their origin, and the ACCOUNT line notes which login a BG or DESKTOP session follows.
  - The card's STOP, `POST /api/fleet/:registration/stop` and FLEET PLAN execution (STOP, RESTART, REFRESH, stopping on RETIRE) check that the origin is `background`; other origins are refused with their manual steps (close in the Claude app, `/exit` in the terminal, `/clear` and paste the CREW BRIEFING).
  - `GET /api/dispatch/brief` has `delivery` per proposal AIRCRAFT. DISPATCH cards and IN FLIGHT rows show `MODE <m> ≠ OCC <m>` when the AIRCRAFT's permission mode differs from OCC's, because the session may hold the FLIGHT PLAN for its user's approval. It doesn't block.
