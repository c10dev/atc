### Added
- Two condition alerts for the 2026-09-30 incident, where the host ran out of memory and every control session died without a word from atc. `control|down|<session>` now fires for any cause: a configured control session (TOWER, OCC, MCC, REVIEW) with no live row is CAUTION, WARNING when TOWER or MCC is down or two or more are down at once, unless the SUPERVISOR's own STOP is the latest thing that happened to it; it clears by itself when the session is back. `host|memory` reads `/proc/meminfo` and `/proc/vmstat`: CAUTION while available memory is under 10% and swap used is over 80%, WARNING for 30 minutes after the `oom_kill` counter rises. Both are read-only (no new state file), go to `alerts`, and keep stable keys so the browser, SwiftBar and ANNUNCIATOR show them with no client change (ATC-203, [docs/alerting.md](docs/alerting.md) "A1b as built").
