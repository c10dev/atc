### Added
- AIRCRAFT never ask a person: every AIRCRAFT LAUNCH carries an atc policy hook (`hooks/policy.mjs`, a `PermissionRequest` hook added with `claude --bg --settings`). It allows known-safe actions inside the STAND and denies the rest, including writes to the Claude config dir, and records each denial by class in `policy-denials.jsonl` (ATC-369, [docs/fleet.md](docs/fleet.md)). FLEET shows PENDING (should be 0) and denials by class.
- STALE STOP: the server stops an AIRCRAFT whose FLIGHT is merged or ARRIVED and that has been PENDING or HUNG for 30 minutes, and records it. On by default; only the SUPERVISOR can turn it off (Settings → OPERATIONS, no `atcctl` command).

### Changed
- The AIRCRAFT card shows a background job's last `detail` with its age (`… · last known, 17 h ago`), not as current work.
