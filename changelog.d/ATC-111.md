### Changed
- Dark cockpit on RADAR and STRIPS: STANDs whose FLIGHT has ARRIVED or been cancelled (and no busy session holds them) are folded away by default (ATC-111, [docs/guide/screens.md](docs/guide/screens.md)).
  - RADAR hides those STANDs and the AIRCRAFT that hold only them, with `ARRIVED STAND N 숨김` in the toolbar and an `ARRIVED STAND 포함` toggle. STRIPS moves such AIRCRAFT out of their bay into a collapsed `GATE CLEANUP` group; a NORDO on an unfinished FLIGHT stays in NORDO. Display only.
