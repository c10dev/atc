### Fixed
- A `LIMIT` no longer stays forever (ATC-167, [docs/fleet.md](docs/fleet.md) "LIMIT that expires as built").
  - A session that ends its turn normally (`end_turn`) after the usage-limit notice and goes idle no longer shows `cut` `LIMIT`. A reply that stops without `end_turn` is still a cut.
  - When atc has no reset time, a `LIMIT` counts as reset 5 hours after it began: a cut `LIMIT` becomes `RESUME` (ABSENT AIRCRAFT follow the same RESUME card path), any other `LIMIT` clears. A reset that FUEL knows still wins.
