### Changed
- css-lint also counts literal spacing (non-zero px in `padding`, `margin`, `gap`, `inset`, `top`/`right`/`bottom`/`left`), literal `border-radius` (anything but `var(--radius-…)`, `50%` or `0`) and `em` font sizes in every `.css` and `.tsx` under `web/src`. `web/css-lint-baseline.json` holds today's counts and can only go down (ATC-410, [docs/design-language.md](docs/design-language.md) section 5).
