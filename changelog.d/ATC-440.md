### Fixed
- A STAND claim from an ended session no longer counts as a holder. Before, a conflicting or review-flagged PR whose only claim belonged to a dead session waited hours for it: TOWER's GO AROUND and FIX went to the dead session instead of coming back as a holder card. Now `noHolderPickOf` (holder cards, RELAY offers), the `holders` count behind TOWER's GO AROUND, FIX and INFO, and the holder in the PR drawer all skip claims whose session is `dead` (the same rule as the `orphan` alert). A live session's claim still counts, and two live claims still raise `conflict`.
