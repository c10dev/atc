### Changed
- MCC's ESCALATE now counts as its INSPECTION of that head. An escalated PR with no P0 or P1 on its current head is CLEARED instead of "waiting for the MCC INSPECTION", so it appears as LANDING in the SUPERVISOR QUEUE, gets the MERGE button in the PR drawer and is counted by the leak counter. With a P0 or P1, MCC also records a `findings` INSPECTION on the head, so the PR shows its findings and gets a FIX. The ESCALATE stays when the head moves, and MCC inspects the new head. Nothing changes in who merges (ATC-390).
