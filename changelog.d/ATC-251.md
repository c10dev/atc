### Added
- Cross-ACCOUNT delivery warnings (ATC-251, [docs/accounts.md](docs/accounts.md) 5.6). `SendMessage` reaches only sessions on the same ACCOUNT (probe recorded), so with LAUNCH ACCOUNTs split (AIRCRAFT `acct-1`, control `acct-3`) OCC could not deliver any FLIGHT PLAN. The settings window and the FLEET header now warn when the two LAUNCH ACCOUNTs differ, FLEET also names running AIRCRAFT on another ACCOUNT than OCC, and the DISPATCH card shows `ACCOUNT 불일치`.
  - `dispatch release` (`POST /api/dispatch/proposals/<id>/release`) for an AIRCRAFT on another ACCOUNT than OCC answers 409 with the reason and produces no FLIGHT PLAN, for a first send and a resend. An unknown ACCOUNT never blocks, and a `launch` card is exempt. Guards and APPLY NOW are unchanged.
  - `PUT /api/fleet/launch-account` now also returns `launchAccountWarnings`.
