### Changed
- FLEET card: LAUNCH in one click (ATC-310, [docs/guide/fleet.md](docs/guide/fleet.md)). The card's LAUNCH starts the session at once with the defaults the server would use (permission mode `auto`, the LAUNCH MODEL, the LAUNCH ACCOUNT or home), and a faint caption beside it shows what that is (`acct-1 · opus · auto`). The old second box under the card is gone. Options (permission mode, model, ACCOUNT) open from a small `▾` joined to LAUNCH, inside the card under the header, with one `LAUNCH with these options` button; Esc closes it and returns focus to `▾`, and only one LAUNCH action is visible at a time. A refused LAUNCH (cap, already running, ACCOUNT not logged in …) shows the server's reason as one dismissible line in the card's alert band, and a refused default ACCOUNT disables LAUNCH and says why in the caption. CREW BRIEFING, which still opens under the card, now uses the full row width without the amber bar. The launch route, the refusals in `launchPlanOf` and every cap are unchanged.
