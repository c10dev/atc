### Fixed
- An approved ASSIGN card whose AIRCRAFT has no live session no longer sits `approved`: the server turns it into a launch card and LAUNCHes under the same limits as a launch card (`ATC_MAX_LAUNCHED`, FUEL hold, stuck LAUNCH, wait after a failure, daily limit), and the FLIGHT PLAN follows. If it cannot launch within `approvedWaitMin` (default 15 minutes after approval) the card is closed with the reason and the FLIGHT goes back to the planner. The DISPATCH brief counts the waiting and overdue cards as `approvedNoSession` (ATC-388).
