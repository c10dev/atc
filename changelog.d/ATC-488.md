- Double filing of a work order is caught: `duty linear create` refuses a near-duplicate of an open ATC issue with `409` and the existing key in every DUTY turn (`--same-title-ok` overrides and is counted; Canceled and Duplicate twins do not count), and a PARKED row shows `possible duplicate of ATC-n` for issues filed through MCP. Switch `duplicateTitle` (settings window, on by default, SUPERVISOR only) removes both; the PARKED section counts refusals, overrides and "both fired" over 7 days ([docs/dispatch.md](docs/dispatch.md)).
