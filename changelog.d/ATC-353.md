### Changed
- FLIGHT PLANs, CLEARANCEs and RELAYs are addressed to the live session at send time, not to a stored name (ATC-353, [docs/dispatch.md](docs/dispatch.md)).
  - The recipient is found by session id, job id or REGISTRATION; a PR or FLIGHT title is refused. Send answers and TOWER's RELAY brief carry optional `sendToId`, `sendToJobId` and `sendToAccount`. Message wording is unchanged.
  - A failed delivery is recorded with its cause; the `UNDELIVERED` hand-delivery card still shows at once. `POST /api/clearances` can take a `stand` without `to` and uses the STAND's live holder.
  - Records only gain optional fields (`cause`, `toSessionId`, `toJobId`, `toAccount`). The daily READABILITY record counts undelivered calls by cause.
