### Added
- FOLLOW alerts (F3, ATC-278): a new `follow` alert group for bundles in `follow.json`. A READY row and a followed row's proposal waiting for approval go to the QUEUE (the proposal no longer also gets a `pending` line), a stuck row (FLIGHT FOLLOWING `warn` issues) and a failure (GO AROUND, RTS ROLLBACK, a reverted ON) go to ALERTS, and landed (ON) and deployed (IN) go to the LOG. One switch in the alert settings, default on.
