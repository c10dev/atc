### Fixed
- RADIO no longer loads 25 seconds late: `/api/events` now sends one `ping` at once on every connect (before the listeners are added), so a stream with no initial event (`topics=radio`) gets its first event immediately, which the atc-app RADIO monitor waits for (ATC-210, [docs/mac-app.md](docs/mac-app.md)).
  - The 25-second `ping` loop, the event names, data shapes and topics are unchanged; `ping` has empty data and no client treats it as a snapshot or an alert.
  - The web RADIO tab also loads the list once on mount instead of waiting for the connection to open; the reload on (re)connect stays and duplicates are merged.
