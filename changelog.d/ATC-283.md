### Changed
- Design language L5a (ATC-283, [docs/design-language.md](docs/design-language.md) "L5a as built"). Body text is 14 px and captions 12 px in the default density (the compact density keeps 13 and 11). New motion tokens `--dur-fast` (120 ms), `--dur-base` (200 ms) and `--ease` for hover, press and open/close transitions; the repeating liveness animations keep their own timing. Numbers that change or sit in columns (header readouts and clock, STRIPS, FLEET rows and card, DISPATCH, SCHEDULE) use tabular figures so a changing number does not shift its neighbours; a `.tn` utility is available. Colours, layout and structure are unchanged.
