### Changed
- Control sessions end the turn instead of blocking: the TOWER and MCC manuals now say that waiting for a team's READBACK, UNABLE or STANDBY, or for the SUPERVISOR to merge a `user` tier or ESCALATEd PR, is not a reason to stop, and that "report to the SUPERVISOR" means an ATC LOG or MCC LOG line. The RULE BREACH WARNING stays for a control session that really waits for a K1–K3 decision ([docs/alerting.md](docs/alerting.md)).

### Added
- CLEARANCE MOOT: the tick brief lists open CLEARANCEs whose FLIGHT has PRs that are all merged or closed (`clearances.moot`); TOWER cancels them. A SUPERVISOR-only switch (default on) turns the list off; a cancelled CLEARANCE that gets a new CLEARANCE on the same FLIGHT within 24 h, or whose PR reopens, counts as a misfire.
