### Added
- OOOI milestones per FLIGHT (ATC-123, [docs/dispatch.md](docs/dispatch.md) "FLIGHT FOLLOWING: milestones as built"): OUT (DEPARTED), OFF (PR opened), ON (merged) and IN (in service after RTS), as actual times from records atc already has.
  - A pure `milestonesOf(flight, sources)` reads the DEPARTURE LOG, proposals, open PRs, LOGBOOK, `landing.requested` events and `rts.jsonl` (read-only, format unchanged). IN is the first successful RTS whose target contains the merge commit, and only for the AIRPORT whose repository RTS deploys; a missing source is `null`, never a guess. A revert keeps ON and is stated in the FIDS tooltip.
  - FLIGHT FOLLOWING shows `OUT 03:12 · OFF 03:40 · ON 04:02 · IN 04:07` (empty slots for milestones not reached). FIDS shows the latest one beside REMARKS and all four in the row tooltip. `GET /api/milestones` and `milestones` on `GET /api/following` carry them.
  - The FLIGHT RECORDER gets one `kind: "milestone"` line per FLIGHT and milestone the first time atc sees it (`milestone` = `out|off|on|in`, `at`, `seenAt`), built from the records on the next pass.
