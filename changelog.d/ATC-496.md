### Changed
- The DUTY context CAP is no longer a fixed 250k. `duty.json` accepts `cap` (50,000 to 1,000,000 tokens); without it the CAP is 1,000,000 when DUTY runs on a `[1m]` model and 250,000 otherwise. `GET /api/duty/status` returns `capSource` and `capNote`, the DUTY head line uses the effective CAP, and Settings → OPERATIONS → DUTY has a **DUTY CAP** row (SUPERVISOR only; each change leaves a `duty-cap` FLIGHT RECORDER line). A `cap` outside the range or of the wrong type is ignored and reported ([docs/duty.md](docs/duty.md)).
