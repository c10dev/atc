### Changed
- DISPATCH flies without a human: the server approves every ASSIGN and launch card that passes the planner's filters and the existing caps (FUEL hold, `ATC_MAX_LAUNCHED`, daily caps), with no CROSSCHECK step, blind sample or SUPERVISOR card. A card that cannot go is superseded after `autoCardTtlMin` and the planner proposes again. One switch, `dispatch.json` `autoDispatch` (default on, SUPERVISOR-only in Settings; a damaged file reads as off). RELEASE cards and SCHEDULE drafts are unchanged (ATC-367, K3).

### Added
- MISFIRE: `GET /api/dispatch/misfire` and a block on the DISPATCH tab count, per day, the server-approved ASSIGN cards that were later declined, answered UNABLE, RECALLed, superseded after being sent or found to be the wrong AIRCRAFT, as a share of that day's approvals (ATC-367).
