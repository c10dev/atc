### Changed
- ALERT levels: WARNING, CAUTION and ADVISORY, as on an ECAM (ATC-110, [docs/guide/screens.md](docs/guide/screens.md)).
  - WARNING (red): LOSS OF SEPARATION, STRANDED. CAUTION (amber): AIRCRAFT HEALTH, NO CONTACT, UNIDENTIFIED, and a NORDO STAND whose FLIGHT isn't ARRIVED or CANCELLED (or has none). ADVISORY (grey): a NORDO STAND whose FLIGHT is ARRIVED or CANCELLED.
  - The header ALERTS count and the ALERT ticker carry only WARNING and CAUTION; ADVISORY shows as `+n ADV` beside the count, and the list (grouped by level, then by kind) still holds every alert. Colors come from the theme tokens.
