### Changed
- New alerts are announced to screen readers: two hidden live regions in the top bar read a new WARNING at once and a new CAUTION (or a rise in the atc notification count) politely, and nothing on a poll that changes nothing. The ALERTS readout and the ticker button are named with their count. The ticker now stops after two passes unless a WARNING is on it. The header buttons use the shared `.btn`, and the header CSS moved to `web/src/App.css`.
