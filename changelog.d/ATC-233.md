### Added
- DUTY CHARTER REQUEST routing in shadow (DUTY D5, ATC-233, [docs/duty.md](docs/duty.md) "D5 as built"): an operations request said to DUTY becomes a confirmed CHARTER REQUEST that OCC reads on its next tick, behind a `duty.charter` switch (`off` · `shadow` · `on`). Tier `user` (OCC's guard profile changed).
  - The `charter` draft card has 확정 and 버림 (`POST /api/duty/charters/:draft/confirm|dismiss`, `fromThisApp` only). Confirm writes `CR-n` to `duty-charters.jsonl` from the draft's own text. The card shows `queued (shadow)`, `queued`, `switch is off — kept as a draft`, then `OCC would draft: …` or `OCC drafted S-n`.
  - `schedule brief` gets a `duty` section (absent when off) with the requests OCC has not recorded yet; the text is marked as data. In shadow OCC drafts nothing and records what it would draft with `atcctl schedule charter-seen <CR-n> -- '<would draft>'`; in `on` it handles it like a CHARTER DESK request and records `--draft <S-id>`. `schedule charter-seen` is allowed in OCC's guard profile only (`guard.mjs --occ`); OCC stays fail-closed.
  - Settings → OPERATIONS → DUTY gets the `DUTY CHARTER` switch (`on` has the ⚠ confirm step and shows in the policy line) and a shadow record: requests OCC has seen, its would-draft text, and the SUPERVISOR's own later `NEW` draft. No gate number yet.
  - DUTY has no `SendMessage` and no command to change the switch. Manuals: `occ/` (`CLAUDE.md`, `schedule.md`, both languages), `duty/`; [docs/occ.md](docs/occ.md). Guide page "DUTY 채팅" updated.
