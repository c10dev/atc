### Changed
- FLEET outside the card follows the reference-card rules: the list rows, FLEET PLAN, CHECKRIDE and CONTROL SESSIONS show no coloured bar, chip or badge in a normal state (ATC-317, [docs/guide/fleet.md](docs/guide/fleet.md)).
  - A list or CONTROL row shows its STATUS as a small dot and a grey word, like the card; only the dot has a colour (working green, NEEDS YOU blue, AOG amber, NORDO red). The coloured left bar stays only for NEEDS YOU (now blue, it was amber), NORDO and AOG. The origin chip (`BG`), `SEC`, the LAUNCH ACCOUNT chip, the FLEET PLAN mode chip, health and session-name chips are neutral (grey border, grey text); the FLIGHT key in a row is no longer green.
  - FLEET PLAN proposal rows and CHECKRIDE rows have no coloured bar or kind/status word when normal (LAUNCH, STOP, GRANT, agreed, executed); AOG, RETIRE, REVIEW, BLOCKED, disagreed and failed keep their colour. Buttons outside the card are transparent with a 1 px border, and the shared sub-headings, chips and origin tags use the card's sizes. The FLEET card is unchanged (computed styles compared in three themes at 1280 and 390 px). Display only.
