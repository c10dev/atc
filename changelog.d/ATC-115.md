- Working rules: worktrees may also live in the main checkout's `.claude/worktrees/` (Claude Code's worktree tool, branch `worktree-<name>`) next to `/home/c10/projects/worktrees/…`; the main checkout stays untouched (no `git clean`, no `git stash`); cleanup is `git worktree remove` or `ExitWorktree` (ATC-115, root `CLAUDE.md`, [docs/naming.md](docs/naming.md)).
  - Background AIRCRAFT make their STAND with `EnterWorktree name=…` and move on with `ExitWorktree action=keep` plus a new `name`. `EnterWorktree path=` outside `.claude/worktrees/` asks for approval and stalls a background session.
  - `worktree-` branches link the same as `claude/` ones (a test covers the key parsing).
