### Added
- DUTY memory (DUTY D4, ATC-231, [docs/duty.md](docs/duty.md) "D4 as built"): every DUTY turn now starts from what atc knows, and standing decisions survive NEW SHIFT. Tier `user` (`duty/settings.json` gets a `UserPromptSubmit` hook).
  - `duty/brief-hook.mjs` adds the same brief as `atcctl duty brief` to the top of every turn. It is fail-open: if the server is down or silent for 5 s it prints `brief unavailable: <reason>` and the turn still runs. The `PreToolUse` guard is unchanged and still fail-closed. The brief does not reach the drawer or `duty.jsonl`.
  - `decisions.jsonl` (state folder, append-only): `note` (`SD-n`) and `retire` lines, folded by pure `decisionsOf`; a note past its `until` is off. Only the SUPERVISOR's click writes it, through Origin-checked routes (`POST /api/duty/decisions`, `/decisions/:id/retire`, `/drafts/:id/dismiss`); `atcctl` and DUTY cannot. The confirmed text is read from the draft on the server.
  - A `note` draft card now has 확정 and 버림. `atcctl duty card DECISIONS retire` asks for a card that lists the active decisions with 해제.
  - The brief starts with `STANDING DECISIONS` (the last `duty.briefDecisions`, default 20, each with id, text and `until`). They are cut last when the brief hits `briefMaxChars`, and the cut note says so. Alert rows show a STAND name instead of a worktree path.
  - Manual: `duty/CLAUDE.md` · `CLAUDE.en.md` "Standing decisions". Guide page "DUTY 채팅" updated.
