### Added
- METRICS → CONTROL (`#metrics/control`, `GET /api/control-share`): per UTC day and per control role (TOWER, MCC, OCC, CROSSCHECK, REVIEW), the control share of all tokens and the tokens per working control turn (a turn with 3 or more tool calls, the threshold of the SQUELCH opens table). Both come from the transcripts atc already reads; read-only, no switch and no misfire counter. A work order can measure them with `metric: control:share`, `control:tokens-per-turn` or the per-role `control:share-<role>` and `control:tokens-per-turn-<role>` ([docs/control-plane.md](docs/control-plane.md) "W8 as built") (ATC-551).
