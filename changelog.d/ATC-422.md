### Changed
- HOME answers one question, "is there anything for me to do now?". It draws the ATFM banner (when in force), the SINCE LAST LOOK line and one to-do list, which is the SUPERVISOR QUEUE in the server's order. The list sits in a centred reading column (about 880 px, a new exception to design-language principle 8) with no cards: each row is one line (kind tag, subject, what is needed, age, the item's primary button), and pressing a row opens its detail below it (the other actions with their confirmations, the reason, HUMAN CHECK evidence). A WARNING row has a 2 px alert bar on its left edge. An empty list shows `할 일 없음`. The separate QUEUE, ALERTS, STUCK, EFFECT, LATE WAYPOINTS and "LINEAR에서 직접 DONE" sections are gone.
- LATE WAYPOINTS moved to a folded section at the top of the FLIGHTS LIST. The DUTY drawer shows one line, `할 일 n — HOME에서`, instead of listing the queue. HOME's sidebar filters the list by kind (전체, QUEUE, ALERT, STUCK, EFFECT, DONE) with counts, as `#home/<kind>`. New shared `TodoRow` and `SectionHead` in `web/src/kit/`; HOME no longer imports another screen's stylesheets (ATC-422).
