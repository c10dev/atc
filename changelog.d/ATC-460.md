### Changed
- APPLY NOW no longer lets a canceled FLIGHT hold an AIRCRAFT: a STAND claim or open PR of a FLIGHT canceled in Linear does not count, so an AIRCRAFT whose only FLIGHTs are canceled is `move-now` (immediate run and pending tick). FLEET shows such a FLIGHT as `CANCELED` instead of flying. An open PR (Draft or not) of a canceled FLIGHT is a HOME ALERT item that names the PR, the FLIGHT and the AIRCRAFT holding the STAND; atc does not close it ([docs/accounts.md](docs/accounts.md) 5.5).
