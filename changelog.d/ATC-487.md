### Added
- The RELEASE screen shows hand-filed Backlog issues in a folded `PARKED` section (closed, count in the header): candidate-team issues with no blockers that are not a parent, not finished and not an atc proposal. Each row has key, title, priority, age, who filed it, K lines, the `Sequence:` line and the same **fire** button as a READY row (one click moves Backlog to Todo and writes the screen release; same Origin check, priority-0 refusal and K3 path). The section never fires or moves anything. Switch `releaseParked` (settings window, default on) hides it and makes `POST /api/releases/fire` refuse these issues again; the misfire counter counts PARKED fires that became Canceled or Duplicate within 24 hours, over 7 days ([docs/dispatch.md](docs/dispatch.md)). A session that files a work order leaves it in Backlog and does not move it to Todo itself.
