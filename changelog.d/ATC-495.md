### Added
- Removal rule (ATC-495): PILOT'S DISCRETION never covers removing a user-visible feature the work order does not name. Teams keep it or report it in `BLOCKED` and write a `Removed:` line in the PR body (`Removed: none` when nothing goes). MCC INSPECTION escalates a PR that removes such a feature (`removes <thing>, not named in the work order`) and gives P1 for a missing or false `Removed:` line; the packet carries a new `removal` field. DUTY adds a `Must survive:` line to the Context of a work order it fires late. The SUPERVISOR can turn the INSPECTION side off with REMOVAL GUARD (settings, MCC block, default on); the misfire counter and the total of removal escalations show under it.
