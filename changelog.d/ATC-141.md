### Added
- The CAPTAIN report judge (REPORT, [docs/fleet.md](docs/fleet.md) "CAPTAIN report judge, rule first and wider input as built (ATC-141)") classifies a turn cut by a usage limit by rule: `stopped` with `engine: "rule"` and `reason: "limit-cut"`, no Jev call and nothing sent. `server/judges/report-rerun.ts` re-runs recorded judgments in shadow and prints the agreement before and after.

### Changed
- REPORT sends the first 500 and the last 1,000 masked characters (joined by " … ") when a message is longer than 1,500, instead of only the last 1,500. `decision` now means an ask that blocks the work until the SUPERVISOR answers (notes for later are not decisions); `stopped` also names "cut by a usage limit".
