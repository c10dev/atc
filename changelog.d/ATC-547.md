### Fixed
- A DISPATCH card that is about to be auto-approved is no longer replaced by `더 나은 배정` (ATC-547). Under auto dispatch an open ASSIGN card that has settled (or settles before the next planner cycle), or whose FLIGHT was already displaced twice in 24 hours, keeps its AIRCRAFT and its age; the higher-scored FLIGHT goes to another free AIRCRAFT or waits one cycle. A card that must still be displaced records the new card and the score gap, and the displaced FLIGHT's next card keeps the original card's age. The server records each displacement and METRICS → MISFIRE shows the per-day count and the misfires (a displacement whose replacement card was itself superseded or expired).
  - Off switch: `dispatch.json` `contestGuard` (`on` by default; Settings → OPERATIONS → CONTEST GUARD; SUPERVISOR only). `off` restores the old supersede. See [docs/dispatch.md](docs/dispatch.md) "A settled card is not replaced by 더 나은 배정". The new switch declaration makes the PR `user` tier.
