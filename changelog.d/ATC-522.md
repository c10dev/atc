### Changed
- A stuck alert for a Todo FLIGHT that no AIRCRAFT can serve now names the reason (`no-aircraft`, `unqualified`, `no-tail`), the AIRPORT and the missing RATING, and the one next step: LAUNCH an AIRCRAFT based at that AIRPORT (first LAUNCH if it never flew), or for a K3 FLIGHT turn on `k3Relaunch` or STOP a resting AIRCRAFT. Other stuck reasons keep their text. Off switch `stuckUnserved` (Settings → OPERATIONS → STUCK UNSERVED, default on, SUPERVISOR only); `GET /api/stuck-unserved` counts the alerts that used the new text ([docs/dispatch.md](docs/dispatch.md)). The switch is `user` tier.
