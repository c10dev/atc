### Changed
- DISPATCH does not pick a live AIRCRAFT that OCC cannot reach (it runs on another ACCOUNT). The plan marks it unavailable with the `ACCOUNT 불일치` reason, and an open card on it is closed (counted as `wrong-aircraft` when auto-approved) so the FLIGHT returns to the plan. A SUPERVISOR-only switch, ACCOUNT RELEASE (Settings → OPERATIONS, on by default), turns the closing off. The number of cards closed this way is on METRICS → MISFIRE and in `GET /api/dispatch/misfire`; a card waiting on the mismatch shows the reason on the FOLLOW line and in `GET /api/status` instead of only "발송 없음" ([docs/dispatch.md](docs/dispatch.md), [docs/accounts.md](docs/accounts.md) 5.6, ATC-458).
