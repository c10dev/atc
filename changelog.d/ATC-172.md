### Added
- RADIO R3 (ATC-172, [docs/radio.md](docs/radio.md) "R3 as built", [guide](docs/guide/radio-tab.md)). The RADIO tab can read new transmissions aloud through the radio chain: a `LISTEN` switch (off by default, kept per browser), calls only, unanswered only or everything, 1× or 1.5×, SKIP, a queue of 5 that drops the oldest and shows how many it skipped. The phrases are short templates built only from fields (`radioPhraseOf`: "GOLF, Tower, go around, ATC one four seven."); the recorded body, text and message are never spoken. TOWER, OCC (DELIVERY and COMPANY) and MCC (GROUND) each have a fixed voice, changeable in the tab, and each AIRCRAFT gets a stable voice from its callsign. `GET /api/radio/<id>.wav` renders one phrase on demand through the voice cache (`404` when the transmission has no phrase). RADIO speech gives way to WARNING and CALL alert tones, stays quiet in the quiet hours and shows the sound-lock chip. Adds optional `re` and `result` fields to the `GET /api/radio` transmissions.
