### Changed
- FLEET: CONTROL SESSIONS now reads like the AIRCRAFT list (ATC-132, [docs/fleet.md](docs/fleet.md) 8.5.1). It is a second group under the AIRCRAFT rows, with the same row and columns: STATUS (`BUSY`, `IDLE`, `NEEDS YOU`, `NOT RUNNING`), FLYING, loop interval, last activity, FOB and 14-day FUEL.
  - Facts every control session shares (`claude --bg`, permission mode, ACCOUNT, model) are said once in the group header, like `CONTROL 6 · claude --bg · auto · acct-2 · model: TOWER·OCC Sonnet, MCC·CROSSCHECK Opus`; a row shows a chip only where it differs. The per-row ACCOUNT cards are gone; expanding a row (Enter or Space too) shows LAUNCH or STOP, the first message, a muted `STALE n` chip and the ACCOUNT edit. A NEEDS YOU row starts expanded.
  - One ACCOUNT view on FLEET: the FUEL ACCOUNT block (it now also says what a `hold` does). FLEET PLAN's own account line is dropped and returns only for an old server. No API changed; `#fleet/control` still scrolls to the group.
