### Added
- SUPERVISOR RELAY: the SUPERVISOR can send a short English message to an AIRCRAFT from the FLEET card or the PR drawer (`RELAY…`, with a confirm step). TOWER sends it unchanged as a CLEARANCE (`INFO`, or `FIX`/`CONTINUE` for an instruction) and marks it; only the screen can create a relay (`POST /api/relay`, `atcctl` cannot). When it cannot be delivered (no live session, another ACCOUNT folder, or TOWER's `atcctl undeliverable`), the DUTY drawer's QUEUE shows an `UNDELIVERED` hand-delivery card with the text and one step (`CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR=<folder> claude attach <job id>`, open the desktop session, or LAUNCH). The same card covers undeliverable CLEARANCEs and FLIGHT PLANs (ATC-271, [docs/dispatch.md](docs/dispatch.md) "SUPERVISOR RELAY and issue notes as built").
- FLIGHT PLAN and DIRECT assignment text gain a `NOTES FROM THE ISSUE` block: the SUPERVISOR's Linear comments on the issue (newest last, at most 3 comments and 2,000 characters, none from bots). `dispatch.json` `issueNotes` can name the user.
- TOWER manual: a SUPERVISOR RELAY row and the commands `atcctl relay issued|undeliverable` and `atcctl undeliverable`. No guard changed.
