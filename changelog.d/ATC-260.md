### Added
- GLOBE shows FLIGHTs (ATC-260, [docs/globe.md](docs/globe.md)): every FLIGHT in progress is a plane flying a circuit from its AIRPORT and back (departure, turn point, IAF, final, runway, gate). State and place come from the OOOI milestones and the STRIPS progress model: ENROUTE, HOLDING (AIRCRAFT idle with the STAND, an open HOLD CLEARANCE, or a blocked PR at the IAF), FINAL, GO AROUND (a GO AROUND CLEARANCE in the last 30 minutes, or a dirty/behind PR), TAXI, ARRIVED (fades out over 30 minutes), NORDO and BOARDING. Estimates exist only inside the current segment; with no typical duration the plane waits at the start of the segment. No percentage and no ETA. Click a plane to open the FLIGHT drawer; a FLIGHTS list sits beside the AIRPORTS list.
  - `GET /api/globe` now carries `flights[]` (read-only, built from the same data as `GET /api/milestones`). The pure rules are `flightsOf` and the circuit shapes in `server/globe.ts`. Holding and go-around loops move only with motion on.
