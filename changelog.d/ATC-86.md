### Added
- AIRCRAFT health from events (ATC-86, [docs/fleet.md](docs/fleet.md) 8.8 "AIRCRAFT health from events as built"). A turn cut by the usage limit with no error line (Claude Code's `usageLimitNote: "wrap_up"` line, then a normal `Stop`) is now `LIMIT` with `cut: true`: `HOLD · LIMIT (cut 18:10Z)`, a hold like any `LIMIT`, with `until …` from the ACCOUNT's FUEL history when known. After the reset with no new prompt it becomes `RESUME 필요` (ALERT, shown only; the SUPERVISOR sends "continue", atc never messages a team). An AIRCRAFT idle for `stalledMin` (60) holding an In Progress FLIGHT (STAND claim or `tail:` label) with no open PR shows `STALLED`. Any new activity clears all three. FLIGHT FOLLOWING, the TOWER brief and the OCC FOLLOWING manual carry the new codes.
- `hooks/health.mjs` also records `UserPromptSubmit` (clears) and the `quota_auto_resume_*` notifications (time only, `fired` clears `RESUME`). `hooks/` is `user` tier: the SUPERVISOR adds `UserPromptSubmit` and the widened `Notification` matcher to `~/.claude/settings.json` ([hooks/README.md](hooks/README.md)). Without it every state still follows the transcript.

### Changed
- The FLEET row keeps an AIRCRAFT's FLIGHT while it is stopped (cut `LIMIT`, `RESUME`, `STALLED`). Before, the FLIGHT vanished 3 hours after the last STAND claim touch (`ATC_CLAIM_TTL_MIN`), which is how TEAM_G and TEAM_H sat unnoticed for seven hours on 2026-09-28. `GET /api/fleet` lists those FLIGHTs in `flights` with `kept: true`, and every FLIGHT there carries `detail: {commit, pushed, pr}` (the worktree's last commit, whether `origin/<branch>` has it, its open PR). The row shows it as `59a9fdc 7h ago · pushed · no PR` and its status is HOLDING. `flying` and FLEET PLAN are unchanged.
