### Changed
- SCHEDULE is no longer a tab; what is still live moved to the screen of the job it serves (ATC-378, docs/layout.md Y3):
  - `#schedule` opens HOME, as do the queue and menu-bar links that pointed there.
  - HOME shows **LATE WAYPOINTS** (only when a WAYPOINT's ETA is past its target date) and **LINEAR에서 직접 DONE** (approved CLOSE drafts, only when there are any). The S1 ↔ S2 switch is in the BRAKES row.
  - SCHEDULE drafts waiting for a decision are QUEUE rows on HOME with **승인** / **거절** and OCC's reason line, so with the SCHEDULE AUTO switch off they can still be approved or rejected there. TARGET and ROUTE drafts get **동의** / **거절** (a recorded verdict, nothing is written).
  - NEW drafts remain RELEASE candidates; the link now says `HOME에서 승인`.

### Removed
- The SCHEDULE verdict screen (shadow "would approve / would reject", CROSSCHECK and judge chips, the CANDIDATES, IN PROGRESS and RECENT tables) and the READINESS fold (S2 gate numbers, ROUTES WITHOUT WAYPOINTS).

### Added
- Read-only `GET /api/schedule/home` (mode, late WAYPOINTs, CLOSE drafts to finish in Linear).
