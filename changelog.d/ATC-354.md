### Added
- PR holder (ATC-354, [docs/dispatch.md](docs/dispatch.md) "PR holder as built"): when a landing-queue PR has no live STAND holder and a GO AROUND or FIX is pending, DISPATCH proposes a holder as an ordinary ASSIGN card with CROSSCHECK: the AIRCRAFT that flew the FLIGHT if it is free, else a free AIRCRAFT whose TYPE RATING covers it. The FLIGHT PLAN carries the pending text and says not to merge.
  - `rating:SEC` and `Risk:` FLIGHTs only go to an AIRCRAFT with the SEC rating. A PR with no FLIGHT is linked by branch name or listed in the DUTY brief (`ORPHAN PRS`).
  - The SUPERVISOR QUEUE `RELAY` card now appears only when no AIRCRAFT can take the PR.
