### Added
- HOME "no landing for too long" stop rule is now a controlled setting. Each AIRPORT's threshold is the rolling 7-day p90 of the gap between landings (floor 30 min; with fewer than 8 gaps that AIRPORT has no gap rule), shown in the verdict text (`기준 158m`). A SUPERVISOR-only switch `LANDING GAP` in settings (default on) removes only the gap rule; a ground stop and a red main CI still make a stopped verdict. A stopped verdict from the gap rule that ends by itself within one threshold is counted as a probable misfire, shown in METRICS → MISFIRE as a `LANDING GAP` lane ([docs/home-flow.md](docs/home-flow.md)).
