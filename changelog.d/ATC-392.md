### Changed
- Finish before starting (ATC-392, [docs/dispatch.md](docs/dispatch.md), [docs/guide/fleet.md](docs/guide/fleet.md)): in each DISPATCH plan the PR holder cards (the GO AROUND or FIX of a PR with no live STAND holder) are planned before new ASSIGN cards, so a free AIRCRAFT at that AIRPORT takes the open PR first. The RELAY card for the SUPERVISOR appears only when no AIRCRAFT can take it and now shows why (no AIRCRAFT at the AIRPORT, no TYPE RATING, launch cap or busy).
- A holder card that was refused, UNABLE or RECALLed is proposed again for the same head after 30 minutes (no SUPERVISOR card meanwhile); after 3 ended cards for one head it goes to RELAY. A new head is proposed at once.
- The time from a PR going dirty (or getting a FIX) to the holder's READBACK is counted: `holderReadback` in the 2b gate. The OCC manual says a PR holder card is not held for a SUPERVISOR confirmation because another session started the PR.
