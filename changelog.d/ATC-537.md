### Changed
- The 3-second snapshot tick no longer re-reads and re-parses whole append-only files that have not changed: proposals, MCC records, FLIGHT RECORDER day files and landing-gap episodes keep a cache keyed on file identity and read only appended bytes. Alerts and the summary are unchanged. On a synthetic state of prod size (3 MB proposals, 1.5 MB MCC) `tick:alerts` fell from about 130 ms to about 13 ms of synchronous time.
