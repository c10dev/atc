### Changed
- RELEASE answers two questions at a glance: what can I fire now, and in what order does the rest follow. On top, a focal block (`발권 가능 n`, per AIRPORT, issues unlocked by firing, waiting and in flight) and one fire queue per AIRPORT, the only place with fire buttons: READY issues, filed proposals, Todo issues not released, K 효과 확인 and SCHEDULE NEW, in one server order, each row saying what firing it unlocks (`풀림 3 ATC-481 › ATC-482 › ATC-483`). Below, an always-open order map draws each parent issue and chain as one line of chips (`▶` fireable, `○` waiting, `◐` in flight, `✓` released); a `▶` chip jumps to its queue row. 최근 발권 gains 7-day bars by channel, and the K3 HOLD / K3 RELAUNCH lines move into a folded 장치 section that opens when one needs a look. The order tree, `Todo 발권 전` and `K 효과 확인` sections are replaced by the queue and the map. A `K` tag now appears only when the body declares a real K1–K3 effect (`None` no longer counts). `GET /api/releases` gains a read-only `queue` field; the sidebar lists 발권 대기, 순서 지도, PARKED and 최근 발권 ([docs/layout.md](docs/layout.md), [docs/guide/screens.md](docs/guide/screens.md)).
