### Changed
- A theme is now a short list of inputs (background, surface, foreground, the five signal hues, a contrast level) in `web/src/theme-gen.ts`. A pure generator writes each theme's 11-step neutral palette and its semantic colour tokens into the theme's block of `styles.css`; `node web/gen-themes.ts` rewrites them and a test fails when the committed CSS drifts from the inputs. Radar Console, Glass Cockpit and Night Sky are re-expressed as inputs; every generated colour stays within 12/255 per channel of the old hand-set value, so the screens look the same (ATC-439, [docs/design-system.md](docs/design-system.md)).
