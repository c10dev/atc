### Fixed
- DISPATCH LAUNCH loop (ATC-213): a background job that ended idle while `blocked` (no process, no `pid`, no `status`) now counts as a STALE row like a `done` one, so LAUNCH no longer refuses with "already up" and the launch card is no longer proposed and refused again and again. If a LAUNCH is still refused as "already up" and that job is still listed, DISPATCH stops proposing the card for that AIRCRAFT and FOLLOWING tells the SUPERVISOR to stop the leftover job. The ATC-93 guard for freshly launched jobs is unchanged. See [docs/dispatch.md](docs/dispatch.md).
