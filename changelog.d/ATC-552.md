### Added
- SQUELCH switch: the SUPERVISOR sets a control role's SQUELCH `mode` (`off`, `shadow`, `on`), `heartbeatMin` and `fingerprint` (`v1`, `v2`) from the settings window's CONTROL block, so one role can go `on` alone. The route (`/api/squelch-switch`) is SUPERVISOR only (no Origin gets 403), every change is logged in `squelch-changes.jsonl`, and an off button puts every role back to `shadow` and `v1`. A bad stored value reads as `shadow`, 50 and `v1`. See [docs/squelch.md](docs/squelch.md) "Switch as built (ATC-552)".
