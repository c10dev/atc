### Added
- LAUNCH, STOP, the agents list and ENTRY per ACCOUNT (ATC-147, [docs/accounts.md](docs/accounts.md) 5.2): with ACCOUNT folders registered, LAUNCH runs `claude --bg` with `CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR` for the chosen ACCOUNT (the only variable added; still in its own systemd scope, so each daemon lives outside `atc.service`). The ACCOUNT is the request's, else the AIRCRAFT's home ACCOUNT or the control session's label; the LAUNCH panel has a picker (SUPERVISOR only) that shows and refuses an ACCOUNT that is not logged in, at the FUEL hold level, or at its `maxLaunched`.
- `claude agents --json` is read per folder (only while that folder's daemon is up) and merged with an `account` tag; STOP and job state use the session's own folder; RESUME after LIMIT stays on the ACCOUNT of the last LAUNCH. The registry entry may carry an optional `maxLaunched` (off by default); `ATC_MAX_LAUNCHED` stays machine-wide.
- FLEET PLAN ENTRY names the ACCOUNT the new AIRCRAFT would fly on (registered, not logged out, below `holdPct`, lowest use) and enters it with that `account`. The "ENTRY도 제안 안 함 (새 세션이 열릴 계정을 모름)" block no longer applies once ACCOUNTS are registered. Without a registry nothing changes, including the argv and environment.
