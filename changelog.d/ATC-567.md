### Changed
- A CLEARANCE with no FLIGHT whose addressee session has ended (the FLEET's liveness, including a background job that is gone) is closed by the server 2 hours later as `undeliverable` with the reason `addressee ended`, by an appended record line. It leaves TOWER's `clearances.pending[]` and `overdue` and no longer holds TOWER's restart. An original and its RESENDs close together; a chain that got an answer is left alone. CLEARANCEs with a FLIGHT keep the CLEARANCE MOOT rule. Switch `clearanceEnded` (settings window, OPERATIONS, CLEARANCE ENDED), SUPERVISOR only, default `on`; `off` keeps them overdue as before. Under the row: CLEARANCEs closed in the last 7 days and misfires (an answer arrived after the close, or the session came back within 24 h) ([docs/alerting.md](docs/alerting.md) "CLEARANCEs to an ended session").
