### Changed
- RETURN TO SERVICE now also checks that it didn't take the fleet down (ATC-102, [docs/mcc.md](docs/mcc.md) section 6).
  - Before the restart it snapshots the live background sessions; after the version check it needs every one still live, `daemonInService` false and `/api/control/sessions` answering (30 s more).
  - Any failure runs ROLLBACK and stops RTS; the `rts.jsonl` record names the check and the dead sessions as `sessions: [{id, name}]`. Read-only: RTS never stops or messages a session.
