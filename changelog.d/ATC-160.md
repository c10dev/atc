### Changed
- LOGBOOK no longer asks GraphQL for `reviews` and `commits` in the merged PR list, cutting its GraphQL cost (ATC-160). Reviews and commits are read over REST only for PRs with no LOGBOOK line yet or inside 30 days with `rework` still unmeasured, and kept in memory per `owner/repo#N`. With 7 AIRPORTs a run drops from 217 points to 7. The `logbook.jsonl` line format is unchanged.
