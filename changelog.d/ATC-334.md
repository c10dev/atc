### Added
- Agreement-based approval: the server approves a DISPATCH ASSIGN, a SCHEDULE draft or a launch card by itself when CROSSCHECK agrees (ATC-334, [docs/dispatch.md](docs/dispatch.md), [docs/autonomy.md](docs/autonomy.md) C14).
  - Two switches in `dispatch.json`, `autoApprove` (ASSIGN that is not a launch card, and SCHEDULE drafts) and `autoApproveLaunch` (launch cards), each `off` (default), `shadow` or `on`. They are set only in the settings window (OPERATIONS, "AUTO APPROVE", with a confirm step for `on`); there is no `atcctl` command and no other route, so a control session cannot turn them on. `off` changes nothing; `shadow` only writes `would-approve` and `would-launch` lines to the new `auto-approve.jsonl`; `on` approves with `via: "auto"` and the FLIGHT RECORDER says `by: "auto"`.
  - A card qualifies only when DISPATCH is in approval mode, it is open, SETTLED and not HELD, CROSSCHECK says `agree`, it is not in the 1-in-5 blind sample, the OCC has put no caution on it and its ACCOUNT is not under a FUEL hold. Blind, disagree, HELD and caution cards stay with the SUPERVISOR. At most `autoApproveMax` (default 40) automatic approvals in any 24 hours, ASSIGN and SCHEDULE together.
  - A launch card also needs the LAUNCH cap not full, the AIRCRAFT not stuck, no LAUNCH failure for that REGISTRATION in the last 30 minutes (`autoLaunchBackoffMin`) and fewer than `autoLaunchMax` (default 6) automatic LAUNCHes in 24 hours. The approval and LAUNCH run inside the server through the same `approveLaunch`; the launch-card approve route keeps its Origin check.
  - `via: "auto"` is not a human decision: it is left out of the 2b gate, the CROSSCHECK match rate and the one-click rate. The rules are pure functions (`server/auto-approve.ts`, tested), the once-a-minute pass is `server/auto-approve-run.ts`. Settings and state formats are additive (new optional `dispatch.json` fields and a new log), so old files read as `off`.
