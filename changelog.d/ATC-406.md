### Fixed
- The FLIGHT, PR, DUTY and IDEAS drawers and the GLOBE view now behave as real dialogs for keyboard users: Tab and Shift+Tab stay inside, closing returns focus to the control that opened it, and Esc while typing in the DUTY text field leaves the field instead of closing the drawer. On the GLOBE airport view a line can be picked with Tab, then Enter or Space, and has an accessible name ([docs/guide/screens.md](docs/guide/screens.md)).
