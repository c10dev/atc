### Changed
- FLIGHTS board and list (FIDS) are built on the shared table, scroll box, button and segmented control: the table sits in a named, focusable scroll region, and the List / Board switch in the display options is the shared segmented control (arrow keys move between the two). The display-option menu is about 6 px taller and the switch no longer draws a border around each choice; the display-option button takes the shared button look. The departure-board look of the rows, columns and split-flap cells is unchanged, and the FIDS and split-flap rules now live in their own files instead of `styles.css` (ATC-425).
