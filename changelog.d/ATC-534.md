### Fixed
- A background AIRCRAFT whose job process is gone is now `absent` (`job gone (last state 13:16Z)`), not `idle`. Liveness comes from proof of a process (the daemon roster, or a session pid whose `procStart` matches), never from `state.json`. An approved card held by such an AIRCRAFT is counted in `approvedNoSession` and goes down the ATC-388 path: launched again, or closed after `approvedWaitMin` so DISPATCH picks another AIRCRAFT. A LAUNCH that dies at init is recorded (`job-liveness init-death`), and after two in a row the server stops launching that REGISTRATION by itself. New SUPERVISOR switch `jobLiveness` (default on; off restores today's rule), and METRICS → MISFIRE counts the cards relaunched or handed off because of it ([docs/fleet.md](docs/fleet.md) "JOB LIVENESS as built") (ATC-534).
