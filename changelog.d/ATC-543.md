### Changed
- A closed FLIGHT (Done, Canceled, Duplicate) stops alerting through its leftovers: no `no-pr`, `no-arrival`, `no-departure`, `review-no-pr`, `stranded` or ORPHAN FLIGHT. Its leftover STAND goes on the `alert|cleanup` line with its path, change count and unpushed commit count, and with `git -C <checkout> worktree remove <path>` only when both counts are 0 (atc never runs it). A DECISION card whose FLIGHT is closed or whose PR is merged or closed leaves the queue ([docs/alerting.md](docs/alerting.md)).
