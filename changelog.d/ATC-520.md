### Added
- BROWSER GATE: Playwright MCP browsers across all sessions are capped at N at once (default 3). A request with no free slot waits in a short queue and, after 90 s, gets a readable BUSY answer instead of a page-like failure. A Chrome left after its session ended is stopped and its slot freed. Off switch and counters are in Settings → OPERATIONS ([docs/browser-gate.md](docs/browser-gate.md)). It takes effect when the SUPERVISOR installs the generated Playwright MCP config; nothing is changed until then. The change is `user` tier.
