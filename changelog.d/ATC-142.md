### Changed
- Voice callouts follow-up (ATC-142, [docs/guide/voice.md](docs/guide/voice.md)): `ATC_TTS_ENGINE` now defaults to `none`, as the guide and the research notes said, so an install without Piper shows `TTS 엔진 없음` instead of `no-binary`; pick `piper` in settings or in `.env.local` after installing it. `GET /api/voice/alert/:key.wav` answers 404 unless the alert is WARNING or CALL, the same rule the browser uses, even for a CAUTION kind that has a phrase template. The engine picker lists `none`, `piper` and `stub` (marked as a test engine), exactly the values the guide lists.
### Added
- Tests for the voice play sequence (tone, then the voice once; a repeating WARNING tone does not repeat the voice; ACK stops the tone loop and a playing voice; tone only when the WAV can't be fetched), the WAV route's WARNING/CALL rule, and `ttsEngine`/`ttsVoice` settings validation.
