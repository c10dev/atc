### Added
- GLOBE G7 (ATC-268): radio on the globe and an AIRPORT view. Transmissions RADIO already merges (`GET /api/radio`, the `radio` SSE topic) are drawn between the AIRPORT and the plane: the last 2 minutes as dashed lines with a travelling dot, an open call blinking until its reply and amber once overdue. Pressing an AIRPORT (or its code in the list) opens `#globe/<CODE>`: gates, runway, OCC / TOWER / MCC (and CROSSCHECK once PREFLIGHT traffic exists), planes placed by their scene state, each recent transmission as a line with its `head` only (`body` on click) and a list that links to RADIO filtered to that AIRPORT. Read only; the scene, the data and the server are unchanged. Motion only with `settings.motion` on.
