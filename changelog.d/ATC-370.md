### Added
- SCHEDULE and FLEET PLAN run without a human: the server approves open SCHEDULE drafts of CLASSIFY, TAIL, CLOSE, WAYPOINT and NEW, and runs open FLEET PLAN proposals of LAUNCH, STOP, RESTART, REFRESH and AOG, under the existing caps (FUEL hold, `ATC_MAX_LAUNCHED`, daily caps). A NEW draft creates its issue in Backlog; ROUTE and TARGET changes stay proposals. Two off switches (`schedule.json` and `fleet-plan.json` `auto`, on by default) are changed only in the settings window. Undone work is counted per day as misfires in `misfires.jsonl` and `GET /api/autonomy/auto` (ATC-370).
