### Added
- OCC restart safety (ATC-169, [docs/control-recycle.md](docs/control-recycle.md) section 4, [docs/dispatch.md](docs/dispatch.md) "Arrival reports as built").
  - `dispatch brief` lists `arrivalMissing` (FLIGHTs DISPATCH sent whose PR merged with no arrival report; `due` after 30 minutes) and `restartSafety` (`safe` and `blockers`: no `approved`/`recalling`, no `sent` under 10 minutes, no fresh missing report, no CHARTER REQUEST touched in 30 minutes). Display only; ATC-166's recycle reads it.
  - `schedule wip`, `schedule wip touch` and `schedule wip done` keep a CHARTER REQUEST that is being worked out on the server (`charter-wip.json`, listed as `wip` in `schedule brief`). It is not a draft: no verdict, no release, and it does not count toward the 5 open drafts.

### Changed
- OCC records a CAPTAIN `ARRIVED` report the moment it reads it, before any other step (`occ/CLAUDE.md` and the tick skill). A report that arrives during a restart is no longer lost to a late recording.
- Every CLEARANCE, FLIGHT PLAN, RECALL and CREW CHANGE, and the CREW BRIEFING, asks the CAPTAIN to reply to the session name (`OCC`, `TOWER`) instead of the `from` address: tested, a reply to the old address of a restarted session fails with ENOENT.
