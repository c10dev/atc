### Changed
- Every colour in the base rules of `web/src/styles.css` is now a token in `:root` (ATC-415, [docs/design-system.md](docs/design-system.md) L0). Moved: `.tone-alert`, the ticker and its head, the priority chips, `.los-tag`, the NORDO chip border, the strip rail, the parked and LOS holders, the scope and rail inner shadows, the tilt shade and the ticker mask. The values are the same in the three themes, so nothing looks different. The `css-lint` baseline for `styles.css` drops from 43 to 21; what is left sits in rules scoped to a theme.
- `--magenta` (Glass Cockpit), `--gold` and `--display` (Night Sky) have a `:root` default, so a rule outside their theme cannot resolve to nothing.
- The theme swatches in settings are pinned to the theme tokens by `server/theme-swatch.test.ts`. The Radar Console swatch background is now `#05080c`, the same as `--bg` (it was `#080d12`).
