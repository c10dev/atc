### Changed
- A review lane that goes silent no longer holds PRs for hours (ATC-386, [docs/occ.md](docs/occ.md) section 9.2).
  - atc decides per repository that Codex is silent: a PR waited `ATC_CODEX_LANE_SILENT_MIN` minutes (default 30) and Codex gave no signal on any PR there since it began waiting. From then on the waiting PRs and new heads in that repository go to REVIEW at once instead of after 6 hours per PR; when Codex speaks again new heads go back to Codex. Transitions are kept in `codex-lane.jsonl`.
  - What REVIEW may not take (secrets and key paths, no FLIGHT, and security rules while their switch is off) stays excluded.
  - A PR that clears on the REVIEW lane alone is recorded in `lanes.jsonl` and counted per day; `GET /api/landing/lanes` and the DISPATCH tab ("한 레인 착륙") show it.
