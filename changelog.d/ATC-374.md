### Changed
- The SUPERVISOR summary's `needsYou` now names an AIRCRAFT only when that AIRCRAFT itself waits on a person: a background job blocked past `blockedMin`, a tool approval prompt, or a CAPTAIN waiting for the SUPERVISOR's go. A DISPATCH card waiting for a verdict (ASSIGN or RELEASE, auto dispatch on or off) counts only in `pending.dispatch`. The SUPERVISOR QUEUE's NEEDS YOU and GO rows read the same definition (`server/waiting-person.ts`), so the menu bar, atc-app and the queue agree; a tool approval prompt is now a NEEDS YOU row in the queue too. The field keeps its name and type within `v: 1` (ATC-374).
