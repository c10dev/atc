### Changed
- A session that stays on a tool-approval prompt now escalates (ATC-327, [docs/alerting.md](docs/alerting.md) "PENDING approval that lasts, as built"). The `pending|tool|…` item was ADVISORY forever; it becomes **CAUTION after `pendingMin` minutes** (default 10, `ATC_HEALTH_PENDING_MIN`) and **at once when a CLEARANCE, FLIGHT PLAN, RECALL or CREW CHANGE to that AIRCRAFT is still open**. The key is unchanged, so an ACK carries over.
  - It shows what the session asks and how to reach it: Claude Code writes `state: working` with `needs: "approve Write: …"`, which atc used to drop. `pendingNeeds` is kept for `working` jobs and shown as `PENDING · approve Write: …` (in `--blue`) on the FLEET row, the card's alert band and STRIPS, and in the alert text with the `claude attach` command (with `CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR` for a non-default folder) and the waiting calls (`3 calls waiting (GO AROUND C-0291, FLIGHT PLAN D-0336, D-0340)`).
  - RADIO: a NO REPLY call whose receiver is PENDING shows `receiver waiting for approval since 04:07Z`, decided by the server. Under `pendingMin` and with no waiting call the item stays ADVISORY. Read only: nothing is sent to a session and approving stays the SUPERVISOR's action in the session.
