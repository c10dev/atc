### Added
- GLOBE tab (`#globe`, ATC-254, [docs/globe.md](docs/globe.md)): a read-only orthographic globe centred on the SUPERVISOR's location. AIRPORTs sit at stable places (a hash of the AIRPORT id, at least 9° apart), AIRCRAFT without a FLIGHT stand at their base AIRPORT, and the night side follows the sun. Drag to rotate, wheel or pinch to zoom, pick the HOME AIRPORT, type a location or use the browser's (rounded to 1°), drag an AIRPORT to move it. The location, HOME, moved AIRPORTs and view are kept in this browser only (`localStorage`), never sent to the server.
  - `GET /api/globe[?home=<code>]`: read-only scene (`airports[]` with bearing and distance from the HOME AIRPORT, `parked[]`), no location in or out.
  - The pure maths is `server/globe.ts` (projection, horizon clipping, great-circle helpers, subsolar point, AIRPORT layout). Land is Natural Earth 1:110m (public domain) inside the GLOBE chunk (about 16 KB gzipped); no package and no external request.
