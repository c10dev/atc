### Added
- K3 RELAUNCH: when a released K3 FLIGHT has no ABSENT AIRCRAFT to take it, FLEET PLAN can propose "STOP <REGISTRATION> and LAUNCH it for <FLIGHT>" for an idle AIRCRAFT (no open PR, no STAND, not at LIMIT, not under a FUEL hold). Approving it STOPs the AIRCRAFT and LAUNCHes it with the FLIGHT's K3 allow; both FLIGHT RECORDER lines carry the card id. The switch `k3Relaunch` is off by default and is changed only in the settings window; the RELEASE header counts approved, expired, rejected cards and STOPs not followed by a LAUNCH ([docs/autonomy.md](docs/autonomy.md), [docs/fleet.md](docs/fleet.md)).

### Changed
- DISPATCH says "K3: needs a fresh LAUNCH" (with the fix) for a released K3 FLIGHT that no ABSENT AIRCRAFT can take, instead of only `no-aircraft` ([docs/dispatch.md](docs/dispatch.md)).
- The FLEET LAUNCH panel now gives a FLIGHT typed into it the same K3 entries as a DISPATCH launch card, when its release is on the screen or in DUTY chat.
