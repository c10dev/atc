### Added
- EFFECT CHECK: work orders gain a `## Measure` part (`metric: <source>:<name>`, `direction: down|up`, `window: <n>d`, or `None`). Once the window has passed after a FLIGHT is deployed, the server compares the same window before and after from records atc already keeps (leaks, misfires, alerts, CLEARANCEs) and writes one verdict per FLIGHT (`improved`, `not improved`, `worse`, `too little data`) to `effect-verdicts.jsonl`. Verdicts show in the FLIGHT drawer and, when bad, on HOME and in DUTY REVIEW. The SUPERVISOR can mark a verdict wrong (the misfire counter). One switch, `effect-check.json` (default on, Settings → OPERATIONS). `GET /api/effect`, `POST /api/effect/mark`. Leak records now carry the release id of the FLIGHT they held (ATC-402).
