### Changed
- Text that sessions send each other is English (ATC-126, root `CLAUDE.md` "Radio"). Korean stays for what the SUPERVISOR reads: the conversation with the user, screens and docs, and the logs a control session leaves.
  - atc builds these texts in English: CREW BRIEFING, FLIGHT PLAN (labels `Goal:`, `Done when:`, `Constraints:`, the PILOT'S DISCRETION and finish lines), RECALL, CREW CHANGE, the closing line of every message, the direct assignment from `GET /api/dispatch/flight/<key>/brief`, and the LAND text. The `[DISPATCH D-xxxx]`, `[OCC CC-xxxx]` and `[ATC C-xxxx]` headers and `READBACK`, `UNABLE`, `STANDBY`, `ROGER` are unchanged, so no guard changes. The issue-body headings atc looks for (`목표`, `완료 기준`, `이 작업만의 제약` and their English forms) are unchanged.
  - Root `CLAUDE.md`, the `atc-task` skill and the TOWER, OCC, MCC, CROSSCHECK and REVIEW manuals say which text is English and which is Korean. TOWER resends a CLEARANCE with the prefix "RESEND".
  - A running session keeps the CREW BRIEFING it started with. The new text applies the next time it is launched.
