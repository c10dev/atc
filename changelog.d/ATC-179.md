### Added
- FLEET PLAN REPOSITION (ATC-179, [docs/fleet.md](docs/fleet.md) 8.6): moves an idle AIRCRAFT's base to an AIRPORT where FLIGHTs wait and no AIRCRAFT is based (STOP, write the new base to `fleet.json`, LAUNCH in the target AIRPORT's repository). Pure `repositionOf` proposes one move per target AIRPORT when the AIRCRAFT is between FLIGHTs, its own AIRPORT keeps enough AIRCRAFT for its FLIGHTs and the AIRCRAFT can fly a waiting FLIGHT; it prefers history at the target, then the longest idle. The target repository and the ACCOUNT are checked before the STOP, and one `fleet` `reposition` line is written beside `stop` and `launch`.
  - Switch `reposition` in `fleet-plan.json` (settings window, AUTOMATION tab; SUPERVISOR only; change recorded): `off`, `shadow` (default, records `would` only), `approval` (a FLEET PLAN card), `auto` (⚠, same confirm as CONTROL RECYCLE `on`; stays off after deploy). `auto` moves at most 4 AIRCRAFT a day, one move per AIRCRAFT per `minDwellMin`, raises an ADVISORY alert for every move, and an AIRCRAFT that would go back to its previous base inside `minDwellMin` turns the mode back to `approval` with an ADVISORY alert.
  - A failed LAUNCH after the base was written keeps the new base and raises a CAUTION alert. The AIRCRAFT card in the FLEET tab shows the last REPOSITION next to the base.
