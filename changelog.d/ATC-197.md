### Added
- ALERTING A1: every SUPERVISOR alert now carries `dest` (`alerts`, `queue` or `log`) on `/api/supervisor-alerts` and the `alert` event, from one pure `destOf` (ATC-197, [docs/alerting.md](docs/alerting.md) "A1 as built"). Keys are unchanged and clients ignore the field until the next steps.
- Three condition items built from state, each clearing by itself, shown in the BELL as ordinary conditions until the header change:
  - `rts|halted` (WARNING): RTS is stopped after a ROLLBACK, until the MCC mode is picked again.
  - `control|down|<session>` (CAUTION): a control session that a RECYCLE stopped is not running again.
  - `reposition|stuck|<aircraft>` (CAUTION): the base moved but the LAUNCH failed and the AIRCRAFT is not running.
