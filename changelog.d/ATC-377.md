### Added
- HOME (`#home`): one screen for what still needs the SUPERVISOR. The SUPERVISOR QUEUE (with Approve / Reject on DISPATCH cards when auto dispatch is off), WARNING and CAUTION alerts, stuck FLIGHT lines with CANCEL and RECALL, and an always-present neutral row of brakes: GROUND STOP and manual departure stop (ATFM), STOP ALL, the automation switches' state and the DISPATCH 2a/2b switch. When nothing is abnormal only the brakes row shows. The FLIGHT drawer gets an assignment history (`배정 기록`) with the proposal's stage times and its brakes, and FOLLOW rows get CANCEL and RECALL (ATC-377).

### Changed
- The DISPATCH tab is gone and `#dispatch`, with the queue and menu-bar links that pointed at it, opens HOME. MISFIRE moved to METRICS, and STOP ALL moved from FLEET's bulk bar to HOME's brakes. The per-card verdict, CROSSCHECK agree lane, BLIND sample, HELD (PREFLIGHT) buttons, 2b readiness block and DISPATCH-side FLIGHT FOLLOWING block are removed. Approve, reject, cancel, recall and ATFM routes are unchanged (ATC-377).
