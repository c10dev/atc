### Added
- LAUNCH MODEL in the settings window (ACCOUNTS, under LAUNCH ACCOUNT): pick the model AIRCRAFT LAUNCH with, as a default, per AIRPORT, and per AIRCRAFT (FLEET card edit). It is passed as `--model` on every AIRCRAFT LAUNCH path (FLEET LAUNCH, DISPATCH launch-on-approve and RESUME, FLEET PLAN steps, APPLY NOW). Precedence: LAUNCH form > AIRCRAFT > AIRPORT > default > last LAUNCH's model > none. Unset passes no `--model`, as before (ATC-279).
- FLEET rows and cards, DISPATCH launch cards and the LAUNCH panel show `next LAUNCH model <model> (<source>)`. The FLIGHT RECORDER `launch` line records `model` and `modelFrom`, and each setting change writes a `launch-model` line.
### Changed
- A RESTART, ACCOUNT CHANGE, REPOSITION, APPLY NOW or DISPATCH card relaunch reuses the last LAUNCH's model only when no LAUNCH MODEL setting applies.
