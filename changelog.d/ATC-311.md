### Added
- `ui-review` gets a runtime step for layout stability (ATC-311, [docs/skills.md](docs/skills.md)): once the screen renders, open it, click each tab, filter, chip, sort header, row and detail, and flag elements that moved or resized by 1 px or more (a bounding-box diff around each click). Interaction shifts are invisible to the CLS metric, and in the ATC-309 greenfield A/B 7 of 8 dashboards had one while axe, hit targets, focus and keyboard checks found nothing.
  - The probe is `.claude/skills/ui-review/references/layout-probe.js`, plain browser JS for the Playwright MCP `browser_evaluate` (no dependency). Findings carry a likely cause and a fix (column width, inserted content, border or weight on the active state, scrollbar gutter, wrapping, a container that follows its content); an unintended shift of a landmark, column, sibling or container is a Blocker.
  - The step runs in `diff` and `audit` modes and is placed early (the skill had been running at turns 46 to 55 of 53 to 63). Tier `user`.
