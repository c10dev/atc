### Added
- OCC SCHEDULE `WAYPOINT` operation and a notice for ROUTEs without WAYPOINTs (ATC-77, [docs/occ.md](docs/occ.md) "WAYPOINT as built", [docs/routes.md](docs/routes.md) "Step 9 and the ROUTE notice as built"). A FLIGHT with no milestone was missing from the ROUTE MAP's WAYPOINT counts and ETAs and got no DISPATCH `waypoint` bonus.
  - `atcctl schedule draft WAYPOINT <FLIGHT> <milestone name or id> -- <reason>`. atc refuses it when the FLIGHT is closed or already on a milestone, when the milestone is not of the FLIGHT's project or is passed (`done`), when the ROUTE has a milestone whose issue list was truncated, or when the milestones couldn't be read. Any FLIGHT that isn't closed qualifies, In Progress included. Membership is read from the milestone side; the issue query is unchanged (`server/schedule-waypoint.ts`).
  - Release is one `save_issue {id, milestone: <id>}` plus the `[OCC S-xxxx]` comment; no labels, state or assignee, and milestones are never created, renamed or reordered. linear-guard is unchanged and passes only that exact input (new test in `occ/mcp-guard.test.mjs`). APPLIED when the milestone read shows the FLIGHT on it; SUPERSEDED when the FLIGHT closes, lands on another milestone, the milestone disappears, or it is passed before release.
  - `schedule brief` lists `candidates.waypoint`: per ROUTE with WAYPOINTs not yet passed, those WAYPOINTs with their exit criteria and the open FLIGHTs on none of them. ROUTEs with only passed WAYPOINTs or a truncated milestone give none. atc never drafts from it; OCC drafts at most 2 per pass, only when one WAYPOINT's exit criteria clearly cover the FLIGHT.
  - `schedule brief` has `routesWithoutWaypoints` (ROUTE, open FLIGHT count, `fresh`, `reportedAt`). OCC reports each new one to the SUPERVISOR once and runs `atcctl schedule route-ack` (`POST /api/schedule/routes/ack`, kept in `routes-without-waypoints.json`); a ROUTE that gets a WAYPOINT or runs out of open FLIGHTs is forgotten.
  - The SCHEDULE tab shows WAYPOINT cards like PRIORITIZE, a WAYPOINT candidate list and ROUTES WITHOUT WAYPOINTS; WAYPOINT verdicts count toward the S2 gate. The OCC SCHEDULE procedure has "WAYPOINT 전에" and "WAYPOINT 없는 ROUTE", and the OCC rules say OCC never creates, renames or reorders milestones.
  - An older atc reading `schedule.jsonl` after a rollback does not skip `WAYPOINT` lines, as found for `TAIL`: it would close open WAYPOINT drafts as SUPERSEDED on its next brief (and mark released ones APPLIED), without writing to Linear.
