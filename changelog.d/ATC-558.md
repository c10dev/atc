### Added
- Exception judge (ATC-558): a team's UNABLE, a question or refusal without the fixed form, and a second silence on a FLIGHT PLAN or CLEARANCE are decided from a fixed menu (RESEND, HOLD_UNTIL, REASSIGN, ANSWER, ESCALATE, ACCEPT_UNDONE; "none of these" is ESCALATE). Jev answers typed questions first; below confidence 0.8, on an error or without a key, one `claude -p` call decides. An ANSWER is acted on only when the written policy settles that exact point; otherwise the SUPERVISOR gets a DECISION card. OCC and TOWER run `atcctl exception` and carry out the action; the server judges the second silences it sees before waking them. The SUPERVISOR-only switch `judges.exceptions` (Settings → JUDGES → EXCEPTIONS) is on by default; Settings shows the share decided without a control session's judgment, the Jev / Claude split, ESCALATEs and a misfire counter from SUPERVISOR marks. The masked CAPTAIN message, the menu and the policy go to TypeSafe and `claude -p` (K2, approved 2026-10-07) ([docs/fleet.md](docs/fleet.md) "Exception judge as built").
