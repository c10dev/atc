### Added
- DISPATCH marks FLIGHTs that need the SUPERVISOR's go inside the AIRCRAFT, and shows a CAPTAIN's "waiting for my user" reply as a hold with an alert (ATC-120, [docs/dispatch.md](docs/dispatch.md)).
  - An ASSIGN whose predicted paths (ATC-71) include user-tier files (guards, `.claude/`, root `CLAUDE.md`, `.github/`, `package*.json`, `hooks/`, `deploy/`) carries `SUPERVISOR CONFIRM AT AIRCRAFT` with the paths that caused it. Empty or unknown predictions set nothing. The DISPATCH card, its IN FLIGHT row and the approve dialog show the mark, the paths, one line to paste into that AIRCRAFT's session and how to open it (`claude agents`). Approving works as before.
  - `atcctl dispatch await-supervisor <D-xxxx> -- <reason>` records a CAPTAIN reply that is neither READBACK nor a refusal. The proposal stays `sent` and shows `AWAITING SUPERVISOR`; a later `dispatch readback` (or UNABLE, RECALL, expiry) clears it, and it doesn't count as READBACK overdue. FLIGHT FOLLOWING shows it and the AIRCRAFT session gets a health alert once per proposal, like BLOCKED sessions.
  - The OCC manual says: when a CAPTAIN holds for its user, run the new command; don't retry, and don't relay any approval. The go is typed by the SUPERVISOR in the team session. Nothing in atc sends it.
