### Added
- KILL GUARD (ATC-134, [hooks/README.md](hooks/README.md)): a `PreToolUse(Bash)` hook in the root `.claude/settings.json` blocks name- or pattern-based kills of the production service: `pkill`/`killall` with `server/index`, `atc` or `node` in the pattern, `kill $(pgrep …)`, `fuser -k`, and `systemctl --user stop|restart|kill atc`. It explains why and what to do instead, is fail-closed, and lets `kill <pid>` and `atc-rts` through.
- The server prints its `pid`, `ppid` and port at start and logs `SIGTERM`/`SIGINT`/`SIGHUP` before it exits, so `journalctl --user -u atc` shows what happened to 7700.

### Changed
- `deploy/atc.service`: `Restart=always` (was `on-failure`) with a start limit of 20 restarts in 10 minutes, so 7700 comes back after any unexpected exit; a `systemctl stop` stays stopped. **The SUPERVISOR reinstalls the unit once** ([deploy/README.md](deploy/README.md)).
- Root `CLAUDE.md` "검증" and the `atc-task` skill: start the test server with its PID saved and stop it only with `kill "$(cat <tmp>/server.pid)"`; never kill by name or pattern, never touch a process you didn't start.
