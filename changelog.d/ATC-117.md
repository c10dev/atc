### Changed
- OCC notes, BRIEFINGs and CROSSCHECK marks now go only to SETTLED DISPATCH proposals: approved, or open (`proposed` or on HOLD) for at least `settleMin` minutes (`dispatch.json`, default 10; `0` is the old behaviour) (ATC-117, [docs/dispatch.md](docs/dispatch.md) "SETTLED as built").
  - Proposals that get SUPERSEDED within minutes no longer cost a control turn. Sending an approved proposal, RECALL and CREW CHANGE never wait, and the planner, scores, pair rule and FLIGHT hold are untouched. `proposals.jsonl` has no new op: `settled` is computed when read.
  - `dispatch brief` marks each `open`/`held` item `settled` (and `settlesInMin`) and adds `unsettled`; the CROSSCHECK brief keeps only SETTLED items in `pending` and counts the markable rest in `unsettledMarks`. The SQUELCH fingerprints count only SETTLED proposals, and a proposal turning SETTLED changes them by itself.
  - The DISPATCH card of an unsettled proposal shows `메모 대기 (n분 뒤)` where the note would be. Changed control rule: OCC `/tick` step 3 (and OCC and CROSSCHECK manuals).
  - Trade-off: the SUPERVISOR's median approval (3.9 min) comes before `settleMin`, so a fast approval goes out without a BRIEFING or CROSSCHECK mark. OCC now adds a note to an approved ASSIGN that has none, right before `dispatch release` (never delaying the send).
