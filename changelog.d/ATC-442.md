### Changed
- The screen is laid out as a shell (ATC-442, [docs/layout.md](docs/layout.md) section 7, Z1). The tab row is now an icon rail on the left: HOME, RELEASE, FLIGHTS, FLEET and METRICS, each with a tooltip, an accessible name and arrow-key navigation. A count badge shows only what needs the SUPERVISOR (QUEUE items on HOME, READY on RELEASE). GLOBE, HELP and settings moved to the foot of the rail; the settings button is now the SUPERVISOR mark that replaced the ATC brand button. The header is a top bar of readouts with DUTY at its right and a fold button at its left that does nothing until the sidebar arrives. At 860 px and narrower the rail becomes a bottom tab bar with labels. Every address works as before.
