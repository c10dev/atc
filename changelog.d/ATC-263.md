### Added
- GLOBE G3: AIRPORT-to-AIRPORT moves as great-circle arcs. `GET /api/globe` gains `moves[]` (`kind` `outstation` or `reposition`, `aircraft`, `callsign`, `from`, `to`, `t`, `endedAt`, `failed`, `proposal`). An AIRCRAFT working at another AIRPORT (OUTSTATION) gets a dashed arc from its base with the plane at the destination; an approved REPOSITION gets a dotted ferry arc (the plane at the old base until the new LAUNCH, at the new one after; no invented mid-point) that fades for 2 hours when finished, amber if it failed. A `MOVES` list repeats them as text. Read-only; the SPACE theme shows the list only (ATC-263).
