### Added
- A control session that stays absent is relaunched or escalated (ATC-532, [docs/control-recycle.md](docs/control-recycle.md) 8). When TOWER, OCC, MCC or REVIEW has no live session for longer than a limit (default 20 minutes), atc relaunches it through `launchControl`, the FLEET LAUNCH path, if the previous job is proven gone: the host rebooted after it started, its `state.json` reads `stopped` or `failed`, or the daemon is up and has no worker for it, and no process runs in the control folder. Only the production server launches, once per absence and at most once an hour per role; OCC only while `auto.OCC` is true. A SUPERVISOR STOP is never relaunched.
- After a server start, a role that is already absent is judged 2 minutes after the first snapshot instead of 20 minutes, so after a host reboot the control sessions come back without a manual LAUNCH (on 2026-10-07 TOWER, MCC, OCC and REVIEW stayed down until the SUPERVISOR launched them).
- When atc does not or cannot relaunch, a WARNING `control|absent|<session>|<n>` names the session, the minutes absent, the last job and the reason, and repeats every 30 minutes under a new key so the browser and ANNUNCIATOR notify again, until the session is back or the SUPERVISOR acknowledges it. The `control|down` line is unchanged.
- Four SUPERVISOR switches in the settings window (OPERATIONS, CONTROL ABSENT): relaunch (on), escalation (on), quiet-hours pass (on: the escalation carries `passQuiet` and the browser plays its tone in quiet hours; ANNUNCIATOR does not read it yet) and the limit (20 minutes). The block shows relaunches, escalations, the misfires (a duplicate job within an hour of a relaunch, escalations marked false), absent minutes per role, the open absences with ACK and the latest decisions. Every decision and every absence (start/end pair) is a FLIGHT RECORDER `control absent` line; `GET /api/control-absent` returns absent minutes per role per day.

### Changed
- A background row with no `pid` and no `status` that started before the host boot is STALE whatever its job state, so a job cut off by a reboot no longer blocks LAUNCH (ATC-532).
