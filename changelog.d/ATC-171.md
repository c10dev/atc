### Added
- RADIO tab R2 (ATC-171, [docs/radio.md](docs/radio.md) "R2 as built", [guide](docs/guide/radio-tab.md)). A new `RADIO` tab shows the recorded radio traffic as a live text log: pick DELIVERY, TOWER, GROUND and COMPANY (several, or monitor all) and filter by AIRPORT and AIRCRAFT (kept in `localStorage`, `atc.radio.*`). Each line reads `HH:MM:SSZ · FREQ · FROM → TO · head`, the reply is indented under its call, an open call shows its age and turns amber with the text `NO REPLY` after `overdueAt`, the recorded `body` expands as recorded, and each line links to its PR, DISPATCH, STRIPS or FLEET card. New lines arrive over the SSE topic `radio` without moving the scroll when the SUPERVISOR has scrolled up (a "N new" pill). A REPLAY scrubber plays back the last 6 hours at 1×, 4× or 16×. Read only: no buttons that send, ACK or approve, and no audio (R3). Also adds an optional `pr` field to the `GET /api/radio` transmissions of ARRIVED reports and MCC lines.
