### Added
- SINCE LAST LOOK (ATC-383, [docs/guide/screens.md](docs/guide/screens.md), [docs/guide/menubar.md](docs/guide/menubar.md)): one server-computed line of what changed since the SUPERVISOR last looked: FLIGHTs released, landed (ON), deployed (IN), the FLIGHTs stuck now and the items waiting for the SUPERVISOR now. Each count opens its list. It shows at the top of the default tab (HOME once it exists) and as the first line of the menu bar menu, and it is absent when nothing is new and nothing waits.
- The last-look marker is one value in `since-look.json` shared by every client. `POST /api/since-look/seen` moves it forward (SUPERVISOR screen only, up to the time the screen drew the line); the **읽음** button and hiding the tab call it. `GET /api/since-look` reads without moving it. `GET /api/supervisor-summary` gains an optional `sinceLook` field (still `v: 1`, nothing renamed).
