### Added
- AUTOLAND merge review (ATC-328): on an AUTOLAND AIRPORT, a review recorded in atc for a PR head (REVIEW session, `atcctl landing review`, a `pass` or `findings` with P0/P1/P2 counts) is that head's landing review. A `pass` clears `no-review` on LANDING SEQUENCE, the TOWER brief and `/api/autoland`; `findings` shows as `review-findings`. The review carry (ATC-31) applies, the strip shows `MERGE REVIEW: REVIEW pass` and who recorded it, and AUTOLAND re-reads the record right before merging ([docs/occ.md](docs/occ.md) 9.7).
  - Records go to the append-only `autoland-reviews.jsonl` with the time, repo, PR, head, verdict, counts, text and `by`.
  - New `autoland.json` switch `reviewedSecurity` (`off` by default, `delegate`), set only from the settings window (AUTOMATION → LANDING, with a ⚠ line). With `delegate`, a `rating:SEC` or security-gate PR with a `pass` on its head can be merged by AUTOLAND `merge`; `.env`, key, migration and SQL paths, Risk labels and PRs without a FLIGHT are still left to the SUPERVISOR. With `off`, nothing changes.
