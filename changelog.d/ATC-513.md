### Changed
- A CLEARED PR on an AUTOLAND AIRPORT (merge mode) that AUTOLAND hands to the SUPERVISOR is now a SUPERVISOR landing: HOME shows one LANDING row with AUTOLAND's own reason (and, for a migration, `apply on hosted first, then merge`), and TOWER sends no LAND to the holder team (one INFO per head, a LAND already out is left alone and not counted as overdue). Without an AUTOLAND verdict nothing changes. A SUPERVISOR-only switch (settings, AUTOMATION, default on) turns it off; the misfire counter shows hand-offs that were closed without merging and LANDs that still reached a team ([docs/mcc.md](docs/mcc.md)).
