### Fixed
- DISPATCH closes a `sent`, `accepted` or STAND-free `departed` card whose FLIGHT is already Done, Canceled or Duplicate in Linear: new status `closed` with the reason `FLIGHT 상태가 바뀜(<state>)`, shown on the RECENT list and in FOLLOWING. `recalling` cards are never closed this way. A Done FLIGHT with an ARRIVED report (or a landed PR) ends as `arrived` instead (ATC-266).
- `dispatch arrived` now works on an `accepted` card for a STAND-free FLIGHT, or for a FLIGHT that never showed a STAND and has a `dispatch report` recorded (`departedVia: "report"`); otherwise it is refused with the reason.
- OCC manual: after `dispatch report` for a FLIGHT with no PR whose card is still `accepted`, run `dispatch arrived`.
