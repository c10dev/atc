### Added
- OTHER BACKGROUND SESSIONS (ATC-184, [docs/fleet.md](docs/fleet.md) 8.5.3). Background sessions that are neither an AIRCRAFT nor a control session take `ATC_MAX_LAUNCHED` slots but appeared nowhere. The FLEET tab now lists them below CONTROL (name, folder, state, idle for, the job detail, hidden when empty) with a **STOP** button (same Origin guard as CONTROL STOP, `POST /api/control/others/:id/stop`, recorded in the FLIGHT RECORDER as `kind: "other", op: "stop"`; only an id that is read as an other background session can be stopped). Where the cap is shown or refused it names the holders, for example `백그라운드 7/7 — AIRCRAFT 6 · 그 밖 1 (ENGINEERING-NIGHT, 6h idle)`: the `409` of `POST /api/fleet/:reg/launch`, `launchFullWhy` on DISPATCH launch cards and the FLEET LAUNCH panel. When the cap is full and a launch card waits, an other background session idle for more than 120 minutes raises one ADVISORY alert `cap|other|<id>`. `GET /api/control/sessions` carries `others[]`. What the cap counts is unchanged, and nothing is stopped automatically.

### Changed
- `GET /api/fleet/sessions` also returns `holders` and `launched`, and the cap `409` message of `POST /api/fleet/:reg/launch` now ends with the holders.
