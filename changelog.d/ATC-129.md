### Added
- LAUNCH on approve and RESUME cards (ATC-129, [docs/dispatch.md](docs/dispatch.md), [docs/guide/reviewing.md](docs/guide/reviewing.md)). Claude Code retires a background session about 60 minutes after its last turn (`bg retire …: idle 60m` in the daemon log); such an AIRCRAFT now stays a DISPATCH candidate.
  - An absent AIRCRAFT that atc launched before (a FLIGHT RECORDER LAUNCH in 14 days), in the registry and not RETIRED, AOG, `RESTARTING` or held by a limit, gets cards marked `LAUNCH on approve` (`proposal.launch`). Desktop and terminal AIRCRAFT are never launched this way.
  - Approving such a card on the DISPATCH screen launches the session through the FLEET LAUNCH path (`by: "SUPERVISOR"`, `proposal: "D-xxxx"` in the FLIGHT RECORDER) and keeps the proposal approved until the new session is live; `dispatch release` answers `LAUNCHING — 새 세션을 기다림` until then. A failed launch, or no session within `restartGraceMin`, closes the card with `LAUNCH 실패 — …`, sends nothing, and shows a `launch` issue in FLIGHT FOLLOWING.
  - Live background sessions plus approved launch cards without a session never pass `ATC_MAX_LAUNCHED`; over the cap the card says so and approving it is refused.
  - A FLIGHT whose absent background AIRCRAFT was cut by the usage limit comes back after the reset as a RESUME card for the same REGISTRATION, once per cut, with the STAND and branch from the DEPARTURE LOG, the last commit and the last line of the CAPTAIN's report. Its FLIGHT PLAN says "resume, don't restart". A live session in `RESUME` is unchanged (the SUPERVISOR sends "continue").
  - The OCC flight-plan and FOLLOWING procedures cover the new 409 and the `launch` issue.
