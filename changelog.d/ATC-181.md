### Added
- Two-letter REGISTRATIONs after `TEAM_Z` (ATC-181, [docs/fleet.md](docs/fleet.md) "Two-letter REGISTRATIONs as built"). The team pattern accepts one or two letters (`TEAM_AA` … `TEAM_ZZ`; `Team AB`, `team-ab` and `TEAMAB` all read as `TEAM_AB`; `TEAM_ABC` and `TEAM_1` are not team names). ENTRY INTO SERVICE's next REGISTRATION runs `TEAM_A` … `TEAM_Z`, `TEAM_AA` … `TEAM_ZZ`, then none, and retired ones stay used. A callsign is one phonetic word per letter (`TEAM_RA` → ROMEO ALPHA), shared by the server, the screens and the RADIO phrases and voices. Wherever atc sorts by REGISTRATION (FLEET, NETWORK, METRICS FUEL, CHECKRIDE, RADIO) it is length first (`TEAM_Z` before `TEAM_AA`). Existing REGISTRATIONs, `fleet.json`, LOGBOOK and `tail:` labels are untouched, and a test fails if a new hard-coded single-letter team regex appears.
