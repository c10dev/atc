### Added
- The RELEASE screen lists the Backlog proposals that DUTY REVIEW and SCHEDULE NEW filed: still in Backlog, not blocked by an open issue, oldest first, each with who proposed it and when, its priority and its K effects. One click on a row moves the issue to Todo and records a `screen` release in one request (`POST /api/releases/fire`); a row without a priority is not moved and says so. A row can also be discarded (`POST /api/releases/discard`): the issue is canceled and the reason is kept as a comment on it, so the list does not fill up. A Backlog issue the SUPERVISOR made is not a proposal.
- Each waiting proposal is a `BACKLOG` row in the SUPERVISOR QUEUE (HOME) and a leak (gate P16) in the leak counter. A proposal that was blocked by an open issue shows up once the blocker is done (ATC-401).
