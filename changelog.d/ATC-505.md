### Added
- LAUNCH puts a memory limit on the transient background-session scope (`MemoryHigh=20G`, `MemoryMax=24G`, `bgMemoryHigh`/`bgMemoryMax` in `dispatch.json`), so an out-of-memory kill stays inside that scope and not the desktop sessions, the production service or the DB containers. Switch `bgMemoryCap` (settings window, on by default, SUPERVISOR only; off restores the old launch). The CONTROL block shows the OOM kills inside launched scopes over 7 days. Only fresh launches are affected; a scope that is already running keeps its old limits until the next fresh launch ([docs/fleet.md](docs/fleet.md) "Outside the service").
