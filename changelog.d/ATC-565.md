### Changed
- CONTROL RECYCLE names what holds a restart. TOWER's wait lists the overdue CLEARANCEs by id and addressee (`C-1069→951f27c2`, five named, the rest counted); a session that is not between turns says whether it is mid-turn or `blocked` on the SUPERVISOR, with what it asks; OCC's blocks carry their kind (FLIGHT PLAN, unrecorded CAPTAIN report, CHARTER REQUEST in progress). The `recycle|wait` alert, APPLY NOW and CONTROL BULK show the same text ([docs/control-recycle.md](docs/control-recycle.md) 6).
- TOWER CARRY-OVER: an overdue CLEARANCE no longer holds TOWER's restart; it is carried to the fresh session and listed as `carried` on the `control` `recycle` record. Switch `controlRecycleCarry` (settings window, OPERATIONS, CONTROL RECYCLE), SUPERVISOR only, default `on`; `off` waits as before. Under the row: TOWER restarts that carried CLEARANCEs in the last 30 days, and how many were then followed by a duplicate RESEND or a missed RESEND.
- RESEND record (docs/control-recycle.md 2.3 item 3): the TOWER brief ties each `RESEND …` CLEARANCE to the one it repeats, read from `clearances.jsonl` with no new op or command. `clearances.pending[]` entries carry `resendOf`, `resentBy` and `answeredVia`, and `clearances.overdue` leaves out a CLEARANCE whose RESEND (or original) was answered.
- A control session over its context cap that has been `blocked` on the SUPERVISOR for `waitAlertMin` (60 min) gets one CAUTION card on HOME, `recycle|blocked|<session>`, which says the cap is passed and what the session asks. It replaces the `recycle|wait` and `recycle|over` alerts for that session and counts from the job's own `blocked` start, so a server restart does not reset it.
