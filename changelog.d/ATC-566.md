### Changed
- A server-started DUTY REVIEW turn is skipped when the facts it would carry (idle AIRCRAFT and waiting FLIGHTs, open leaks, landing queue lines and blocks, WARNING and CAUTION alerts by key, open EFFECT CHECK verdicts; minutes and context size left out) are the same as at the last review and that review filed no issue and no comment. A change in the facts runs the review at once; the first review after 00:00Z runs even when nothing changed. The SUPERVISOR's own messages to DUTY are never skipped ([docs/duty.md](docs/duty.md) DUTY REVIEW SKIP).
- Settings → OPERATIONS → **DUTY REVIEW SKIP** turns this off (`duty.json` `reviewSkip`, default on). Under it the settings window shows the skipped reviews and the misfires (a skip after which the next review, within 2 hours, filed an issue the skipped facts also supported); `GET /api/duty/review` returns them as `skip`. Each skip is a `skip` line in `duty-reviews.jsonl`, and a comment or update made in a review turn is now a `write` line.
