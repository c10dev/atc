### Added
- macOS menu bar for the SUPERVISOR (ATC-149, [menubar/](menubar/README.md), [docs/guide/menubar.md](docs/guide/menubar.md)): a SwiftBar plugin, `menubar/atc.15s.mjs`, run by the Mac's node.
  - The title shows the WARNING+CAUTION count (`+n` for ADVISORY), coloured by the highest level, and the most-used ACCOUNT's FUEL (`5h 33% · 7d 53%`). The menu lists items by level with their next step (each opens its atc tab), then DISPATCH approvals waiting, the last RTS, and how many AIRCRAFT and control sessions are working.
  - A key that is new since the last run with level `warning` or cue `call` raises one macOS notification. Seen keys live in SwiftBar's plugin cache folder, not in atc state. atc unreachable shows `✈ —` and a one-line hint.
  - Read only: GET `/api/supervisor-alerts`, `/api/fleet`, `/api/update`, `/api/control/sessions` and open URLs. No new endpoint, token or CORS change. The guide says to reach atc through an SSH local forward and not to make 7700 listen on the LAN.
  - The formatting is a pure module (`menubar/format.mjs`) tested on Linux; `npm test` now includes `menubar/**/*.test.mjs`. The SwiftBar render and the notification were not checked on a Mac.
