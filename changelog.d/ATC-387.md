### Changed
- A FLIGHT whose PR is open and only waits to land no longer uses its AIRCRAFT's slot: nothing to fix (no failing check, review finding, requested change or conflict), no open FIX or GO AROUND, not a Draft. DISPATCH can assign that AIRCRAFT its next FLIGHT, which it starts in a new STAND while keeping the earlier one; the FLIGHT PLAN says so. FIX and GO AROUND for the earlier PR still reach the AIRCRAFT (the claim on a STAND with an open PR is kept for a live session) and are handled in the earlier STAND. `dispatch.json` `slots.waitingPr` (default 2) caps the waiting PRs one AIRCRAFT may hold (ATC-387).

### Added
- METRICS → OPERATIONS shows the median FIX and GO AROUND time to READBACK for AIRCRAFT busy on another FLIGHT against those that were not (`Clearance.elsewhere`, `clearances.fixReadback`) (ATC-387).
