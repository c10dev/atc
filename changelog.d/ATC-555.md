### Added
- A FLIGHT PLAN now carries a short hash of the stored work-order text in its header (`[DISPATCH D-0123] FLIGHT PLAN @a1b2c3 · …`), and its last line gives the exact reply, `READBACK D-0123 @a1b2c3`. A READBACK that quotes no hash or a different one is refused with that reply line, so a work order changed after release is caught at the READBACK; a FLIGHT PLAN sent before this has no hash and is read back as before. OCC records the hash with `atcctl dispatch readback D-xxxx @xxxxxx`. send-guard refuses a stored FLIGHT PLAN whose text no longer matches its hash. A SUPERVISOR-only switch `READBACK HASH` (default on) turns the READBACK check off, and the settings window counts refused READBACKs over the last 7 days ([docs/autonomy.md](docs/autonomy.md) "WO-23 as built").
- New lines in `proposals.jsonl`, `schedule.jsonl`, `judges.jsonl` and `fleet-plan.jsonl` carry a content hash of what was judged, `clearances.jsonl` issue lines carry the PR `head` they point at, and these lines carry the FLIGHT's release id. Existing lines are not rewritten.
