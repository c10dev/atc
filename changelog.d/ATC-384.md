### Added
- `GET /api/status` (ATC-384, [server/README.md](server/README.md)): one read-only endpoint for "what is the state?": the SINCE LAST LOOK counts (without moving the marker), the FLIGHTs in flight with their stage, what waits on the SUPERVISOR, the WARNING and CAUTION alerts and the last RTS. `?flight=ATC-n` narrows to that FLIGHT (stage, PR, blockers, next step); `?topic=text` narrows to what matches. The project skill `status` that reads it comes in its own PR.
