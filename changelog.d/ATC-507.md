### Changed
- `restartGraceMin` in `dispatch.json` accepts `0` as off: no AIRCRAFT shows as RESTARTING and approved proposals do not wait on it (as before ATC-91). A negative or non-number still falls back to 30 (ATC-507, [docs/fleet.md](docs/fleet.md) 8.5).
- How long a launch card waits for its new session is its own setting, `launchCardTimeoutMin` (default 30, positive only, never off). Before, `restartGraceMin` moved both, so a `restartGraceMin` other than 30 no longer changes the launch-card timers ([docs/dispatch.md](docs/dispatch.md) 6.4).
