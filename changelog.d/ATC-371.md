### Changed
- CROSSCHECK is retired: it is no longer launched, kept alive or recycled (a session still running is stopped once by the server), and no server rule waits on its marks. Automatic approval no longer skips a card for a missing or disagreeing mark, ATFM A7 is dropped and S3 keeps only the section-citation test, the two CROSSCHECK-match rows leave the ATFM turn-on checklists, and a disagree mark no longer files a PREFLIGHT HOLD. `POST …/crosscheck` answers 410. Old marks stay readable as history (ATC-371, K3).
