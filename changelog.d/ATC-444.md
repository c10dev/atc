### Changed
- The FLIGHT / PR, DUTY and IDEAS drawers share one column docked on the right of the shell instead of covering the screen (ATC-444, [docs/layout.md](docs/layout.md) Z3). The screen keeps working beside it, so you can click another FLIGHT and the drawer's content is replaced; opening one drawer closes the other. From 861 to 1279 px an open drawer folds the sidebar until it closes; at 860 px and narrower it covers the screen as before. Escape closes it (not while typing in a field outside it) and focus returns to the control that opened it. Clicking outside no longer closes it, and Tab is no longer held inside it at the docked widths. Addresses are unchanged.
