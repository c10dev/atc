### Added
- GLOBE SPACE theme (ATC-261, [docs/globe.md](docs/globe.md)): a `GLOBE | SPACE` toggle in the GLOBE toolbar draws the same `GET /api/globe` scene as space. Earth is the home AIRPORT at the centre; other AIRPORTs are planets on circular orbits (radius by the rank of their scene distance, angle from their bearing, so they stay put from day to day); every FLIGHT launches, orbits and lands around its own body (ENROUTE is the orbit, HOLDING a parking orbit, FINAL the descent, TAXI/ARRIVED/BOARDING on the pad, GO AROUND a climb-and-return loop, NORDO grey where it stopped). State, colour, tooltip, link and the lists are the same code as on the globe. A static starfield, motion only with `settings.motion` on. The choice is saved in `atc.globe`; with nothing saved the Night Sky theme opens in SPACE.
  - No new data and no scene change; `server/globe.ts` is untouched. Transfer orbits for OUTSTATION and REPOSITION wait for `moves[]` (G3), which is not on main yet.
