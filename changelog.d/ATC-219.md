  - `atcctl duty brief|flight|pr|card|note|charter`, with `GET /api/duty/brief` and three draft routes that only append to `duty-drafts.jsonl` in the state folder. A card is accepted only for a row that is in the SUPERVISOR QUEUE now; a charter draft must be English. Nothing goes out and no decision is written.
  - Landing tier: `duty/settings.json` and `duty/*guard*.mjs` are `user`, the rest of `duty/` is `flagged`, and `server/duty-run.ts` is on the side-effect list ahead of D2.
