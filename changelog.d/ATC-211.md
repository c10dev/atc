### Added
- STRIPS progress bar: each STAND leg with a FLIGHT gets a thin four-segment bar (work, landing, RTS, in service) built from the OOOI milestones. Past segments are solid facts; only the current one gets an estimate, shown as elapsed time against the typical range of similar FLIGHTs (`작업 42분 · 보통 30–60분 (BUILD·M, n=12)`), amber past the upper end, and `데이터 부족` when there are too few samples. No percentage, no ETA. `GET /api/milestones` now also returns `progress` (ATC-211, [docs/dispatch.md](docs/dispatch.md)).
