### Added
- Radio voice callouts (ATC-140, [docs/guide/voice.md](docs/guide/voice.md)): for WARNING and CALL alerts the browser plays the ATC-87 tone and then a short spoken callout through a radio chain (key click, squelch, band-pass, drive, compressor, hiss). The voice is rendered locally, so nothing leaves the machine. Off by default (settings → 알림 → VOICE).
  - Server: `server/voice-phrase.ts` builds fixed English phraseology per alert kind (callsigns, digit-by-digit FLIGHT keys, `niner`); `server/tts.ts` runs the engine (`piper`, `stub`, `none`; `ATC_TTS_ENGINE`, `ATC_TTS_PIPER`, `ATC_TTS_VOICES`) without a shell, one render at a time, 5 s timeout; `GET /api/voice/status`, `/api/voice/alert/:key.wav`, `/api/voice/preview.wav`; disposable `voice-cache/` capped at 200 files or 20 MB. The client never sends free text.
  - Screen: `web/src/radio.ts` (`radioSpecOf`, one `radio` 0–1 knob), one voice per alert after the tone, ACK stops both, voice picker with preview, `TTS 엔진 없음` state.
  - Piper install guide (user-level under `~/.local`) with the licence note. Landing tier `flagged` for the external command, `user` because `deploy/landing-tier.mjs` changed.
