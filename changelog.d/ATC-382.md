### Added
- FOLLOW follows every released FLIGHT by itself: an `ARROWS` bundle ("SUPERVISOR의 화살표") at the top of the FOLLOW tab shows each released FLIGHT with the same stage dots, "now" text, stuck limits and next chip, from release to IN, with no FOLLOW click. Hand-followed bundles work as before. A released FLIGHT that reaches IN (or ARRIVED when it has no PR, or ON where there is no deploy) gives one quiet notification, and one that passes a stuck limit gives one notification with the next step, through the existing alert channels (ATC-382).
