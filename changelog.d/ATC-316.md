### Changed
- FIDS folds every group behind a count and turns rows into one-line cards on a phone (ATC-316, [docs/guide/screens.md](docs/guide/screens.md)).
  - The list groups FLIGHTs by flight phase (a header with the phase and its count) and the board keeps its status columns; each group shows its first 5 FLIGHTs and one `<group> N more · 전체 보기` row opens the rest in place (click or Enter, folds back, not saved). A FLIGHT that an AIRCRAFT holds or that carries an alert is never folded away. This replaces the ARRIVED-only cap of 10 (ATC-112): an ARRIVED FLIGHT that still has a STAND no longer stays visible on its own (GATE CLEANUP on STRIPS lists them).
  - Below 860 px the list rows become one-line cards (FLIGHT, title, AIRCRAFT, age as `now · 5m · 3h · 2d`); a milestone (OOOI) or NO CONTACT sits on a small second line, and the board columns stack with one-line cards. No horizontal scroll.
  - Static codes are neutral: the list's FLIGHT number in Night Sky, the Glass Cockpit counts and headings; colour stays on the phase name. At most two text sizes on the screen. The AIRPORT code in a row has no tooltip of only a repository path, and the 1,400 `title` attributes are down to about 120. The list/board switch, the display options and the OOOI milestones are unchanged. Display only (`web/src/fids-rows.ts`, a pure function).
