### Changed
- SUPERVISOR alert noise (ATC-152, [docs/dispatch.md](docs/dispatch.md), [docs/occ.md](docs/occ.md)).
  - `no-report` is now an info item (ADVISORY, not counted in the alert title). It appears only for a FLIGHT that DISPATCH sent (its proposal has a `send`) whose PR merged at or after the first recorded arrival report (2026-09-29T08:34Z), and it closes by itself 24 hours after ON. ENGINEERING PRs and direct work with no FLIGHT PLAN never get it.
  - A proposal that stays `proposed`, `agreed` or `disagreed` for more than 24 hours without a SUPERVISOR decision expires with the reason "24시간 판정 없음" (ASSIGN and RELEASE; a held proposal never expires, and an approved one keeps its own delivery rule). The record format is unchanged; the DISPATCH tab shows them under closed with the reason.
  - SUPERVISOR alert texts for ALERTs that carry a STAND path (unattended changes, orphaned occupation, ...) end with the STAND name, so the menu bar and the screens say where. The alert key is unchanged.
  - Menu bar times are UTC `HH:MMZ`, like the atc screens.
  - The OCC `/tick` step 1 now says how to record a headed `ARRIVED` report and what to do with an older headerless completion report; `no-report` goes to the OCC LOG only.
