### Added
- More TTS engines for voice callouts (ATC-143, [docs/guide/voice.md](docs/guide/voice.md)): `espeak` (espeak-ng) and `kokoro` next to `piper`. Each engine has its own installed-voice list, its own timeout (5 s; Kokoro 20 s because every render starts a Python process that loads the model in 6–9 s, and the voice cache hides it on repeats) and renders one at a time. No resident process.
  - Server: `server/tts.ts` shares one runner (`execFile`, no shell, phrase on stdin); a voice must match `VOICE_NAME` and the engine's own list (`espeak-ng --voices=en`, `kokoro-say --list-voices`). New `.env.local` keys `ATC_TTS_ESPEAK`, `ATC_TTS_KOKORO` and `ATC_TTS_KOKORO_MODEL`; paths never come from the screen. `GET /api/voice/status` adds `engines[]` (availability, reason, voices per engine) and `GET /api/voice/preview.wav` takes an optional `engine`.
  - Screen: the engine picker lists `none` and every engine; an unavailable one is greyed out with its reason. The voice list follows the chosen engine.
  - `tts/kokoro-say.py` is the Kokoro wrapper (`--voice`, `--out`, phrase on stdin, `--list-voices`). Install steps, licences (espeak-ng GPL-3.0, Kokoro Apache-2.0) and a first voice per engine in the voice guide. Landing tier `flagged` for the external commands, `user` because `deploy/landing-tier.mjs` changed.
