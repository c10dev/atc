### Added
- GLOBE: FLIGHTs whose landing waits for the SUPERVISOR are marked apart from those waiting for MCC (ATC-300). `GET /api/globe` flights carry `landBy` (`mcc`, `supervisor`, `holder`) and `landWhy` (`user`, `escalate`, `hold`, `mode`, `tier-unknown`, `teams-merge-off`) from the same `landByOf` TOWER uses, cached tiers only. A FLIGHT the SUPERVISOR has to merge gets a cyan ring and `SUP` (GLOBE, SPACE and the AIRPORT view), a reason in the tooltip, and a `LAND BY` line in the FLIGHTS list.
