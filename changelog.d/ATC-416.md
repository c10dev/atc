### Changed
- Spacing and corner radii in the per-screen stylesheets now come from the `--space-*` and `--radius-*` tokens only; off-scale values snap to the nearest step (1 and 2.5 px → `--space-0-5`, 3 and 4.5 px → `--space-1`, 10 px → `--space-2`, 20 and 22 px → `--space-6`), so theme radius overrides reach every corner. The css-lint baseline drops to the `styles.css` sections (ATC-416).
