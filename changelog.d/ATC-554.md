### Added
- A reply to a closed or superseded CLEARANCE, FLIGHT PLAN or CREW CHANGE is refused with "answer the latest call <id>" when a later call for the same subject was sent, so a team that answers a resent call with the old id is not counted as having answered. A SUPERVISOR-only switch `STALE REPLY` (default on) turns it off, and the settings window counts refusals over the last 7 days ([docs/alerting.md](docs/alerting.md)).
