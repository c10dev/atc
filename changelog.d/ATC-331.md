### Changed
- AUTOLAND: a CLEARED PR left to the SUPERVISOR now holds the update queue only for a grace window (default 15 minutes from its `readyAt`; optional `autoland.json` `supervisorGraceMinutes`). After that AUTOLAND keeps updating behind-only PRs, one in flight at a time, and the PR stays CLEARED with a `SUPERVISOR 머지 — … · 대기 15분 지남` tag. A delegated CLEARED PR is still merged first (ATC-331).
