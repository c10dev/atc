### Changed
- An approved launch card for a K3 FLIGHT now re-checks the FLIGHT's K3 entries right before the LAUNCH. If none can be built (release gone, body changed, ticket missing from the snapshot) nothing is launched: the card stays approved, shows `K3 entries not ready — waiting`, and is tried again each tick until `launchCardTimeoutMin` closes it with K3 as the reason. The switch is `k3Hold`; waits count in the RELEASE screen's K3 HOLD misfire counter ([docs/autonomy.md](docs/autonomy.md), [docs/dispatch.md](docs/dispatch.md)).
