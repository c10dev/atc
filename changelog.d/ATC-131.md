### Added
- Settings window: an `AUTOMATION` tab for the SUPERVISOR policy switches (ATC-131, [docs/guide/screens.md](docs/guide/screens.md)).
  - AUTOLAND (with GROUND STOP), MCC and the SHADOW GATE, JUDGES (JEV), FUEL HOLD and REVIEW moved there from AGENTS. A line at the top shows the current policy, like `AUTOLAND off · MCC shadow · JEV off · FUEL HOLD off · REVIEW exclude`, with ⚠ modes in amber.
  - Moving a switch up to a ⚠ mode shows that mode's warning and needs an explicit confirm button; moving down saves as before. Each switch shows only its current mode's warning and folds the rest. REVIEW `deepseek` shows as `sonnet (deepseek)` (stored value unchanged).
  - The settings window reopens on the last tab used (kept in this browser, `display` as the fallback). `PUT /api/settings` and every stored value are unchanged.
