### Fixed
- NEEDS YOU no longer hides a real wait (ATC-138, [docs/fleet.md](docs/fleet.md) "NEEDS YOU as built"): a background job that blocked again without passing through `working` was treated as answered, because `since` is the first blocked moment and its later turns looked like a reply.
  - `settleJob` now compares the last turn with when `state.json` was last written (`updatedAt`, else its mtime), not with `since`. `blocked` is over only when `tempo` is `active`, or the last turn is later than that write and `tempo` is not `blocked`. `tempo: blocked` with a `needs` always shows NEEDS YOU and can raise the BLOCKED alert.
  - The two ATC-133 cases (`tempo` active; a turn after the block) still settle, and a long real wait still alerts. The alert's minutes still count from `since`.
