### Added
- Fixed-field arrival report (ATC-124, [docs/dispatch.md](docs/dispatch.md) "Arrival reports as built"). The CAPTAIN's final report starts with `[TEAM_X → OCC] ARRIVED ATC-n · PR #n` and the fixed lines `TIER`, `TESTS`, `DISCRETION`, `BLOCKED` (`RESULT <link>` in place of the PR for a SURVEY or CHECK without one). Root `CLAUDE.md` "교신" and the `atc-task` skill section 8 define it.
  - `atcctl dispatch report <D-xxxx|ATC-n> --pr <n> --tier <t> --tests <p/t> --discretion <n> --blocked <none|text>` records the fixed fields as a `report` op in `arrival-reports.jsonl` (`POST /api/dispatch/report`, `GET /api/dispatch/reports`). It works for a FLIGHT PLAN and a direct assignment. The free summary is never stored, and atc does not read team messages: the receiving session (OCC, or ENGINEERING) records the report.
  - FLIGHT FOLLOWING gets `no-report` (a PR merged more than 30 minutes ago with no recorded report) and `blocked-report` (a recorded report with `BLOCKED` other than `none`, visible for a day).
  - The OCC manual and its `/tick` procedures say to record a report when it arrives. `docs/guide/radio.md` shows the format.
