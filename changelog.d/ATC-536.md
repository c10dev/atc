### Added
- A red main that AUTO-REVERT holds because the head is a human merge now raises one SUPERVISOR QUEUE line (NEEDS YOU, `MAIN RED <AIRPORT> <sha>`) naming the failing check, the head, the human merge and the open PRs failing the same check. One line per head; it closes by itself when main turns green or a new head replaces it, and it reverts, merges and sends nothing. A SUPERVISOR-only switch `RED MAIN` (default on) turns it off, and its settings block counts lines raised and closed by themselves ([docs/autonomy.md](docs/autonomy.md)).
