### Added
- LAUNCH ACCOUNT APPLY NOW (ATC-244, [docs/accounts.md](docs/accounts.md) 5.5): an **APPLY NOW** button beside the LAUNCH ACCOUNT dropdowns (settings window, ACCOUNTS) and in the FLEET header moves running sessions to the chosen ACCOUNT after one confirmed click. The confirm lists the sessions grouped as move now, after FLIGHT, wait for a safe moment, and skipped (with reasons), and warns that every moved session starts with a cold prompt cache.
  - Only safe sessions move, one at a time, with the plan re-read before each STOP: idle AIRCRAFT between FLIGHTs (the ACCOUNT CHANGE rules: no FLIGHT held or kept, no open PR, not given a FLIGHT) through the ACCOUNT CHANGE executor, and control sessions at a safe moment through the CONTROL RECYCLE relaunch with the target ACCOUNT. It stops at the first refusal and leaves the rest running. Desktop and terminal sessions, DUTY and a refusing target ACCOUNT (logged out, FUEL hold, `maxLaunched`) are skipped.
  - Busy AIRCRAFT and control sessions not yet safe wait as a pending APPLY (`apply-now.json`, ticked with CONTROL RECYCLE) until the LAUNCH ACCOUNT changes, 24 hours pass, or the SUPERVISOR cancels.
  - `GET /api/fleet/apply-now` (plan) and `POST /api/fleet/apply-now` (SUPERVISOR only; the confirmed LAUNCH ACCOUNT must still be the current one). FLIGHT RECORDER: `account-change` per AIRCRAFT, the `recycle` line per control session, one `apply-now` summary.
