### Added
- DUTY ACCOUNT in the settings window (ATC-242, [docs/duty.md](docs/duty.md)). Settings → OPERATIONS → DUTY has a dropdown for the ACCOUNT DUTY runs on (`PUT /api/settings` `dutyAccount`, registered labels only; not logged in is disabled, FUEL hold is selectable with a warning). A change applies from the next message and starts a fresh DUTY conversation on the new ACCOUNT (`--resume` does not cross config folders): a running turn finishes on the old ACCOUNT, the brief and decisions carry over, and `duty.jsonl` records the change. A saved label that is no longer registered falls back to `~/.claude` with a warning.
