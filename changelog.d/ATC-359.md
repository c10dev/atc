### Changed
- The English that the server sends to sessions (FLIGHT PLAN, RECALL, CLEARANCE closing lines, CREW CHANGE, GO AROUND, LAND text, DIRECT brief lines, landing block notes) now follows a short plain-English style: one idea per sentence, orders in the imperative, full stops instead of dashes. Heads, ids and READBACK, UNABLE, STANDBY and ROGER phraseology are byte-identical, and a test compares them with the text from before the change. The style is in `docs/message-style.md`, with the READABILITY baseline and what the 7-day after-readout compares (ATC-359).
