### Changed
- Waits on the web screen read as atc's own instrument: a short run of sequenced approach lights (`kit/Loading`) sits next to the loading text on the loading lines, the tab loader, the DUTY reply line and the LAUNCH button, and the first load shows a pilot's-eye final-approach scene. Both appear only when the wait passes about 300 ms; the threshold lights turn `--radar` as the first snapshot arrives. With motion off every light stops dimly lit and the text stays. Design-language principle 10 now lists loading as allowed motion (ATC-453, [docs/design-language.md](docs/design-language.md)).
