### Added
- ADD ACCOUNT in the settings window (AGENTS → ACCOUNTS): give a label and atc creates `~/.claude-<label>`, copies `~/.claude/settings.json` into it (statusline, hooks, permissions, `env` minus the keys you untick; only key names are shown), and registers it, together with `~/.claude` under its label when that is not registered yet. A folder with its own hooks, `env` or permissions keeps its `settings.json`. Login and onboarding stay in the terminal; the page shows the command (ATC-186, [docs/accounts.md](docs/accounts.md)).
