### Changed
- Design language L5b (ATC-287, [docs/design-language.md](docs/design-language.md) 3.5 Craft): the FLEET AIRCRAFT card is the reference card. A normal card has no colour: status is a small dot plus the word (only the dot is coloured), tags (origin, AIRPORT, STALE) and the `SEC` TYPE RATING chip are neutral, and FLYING / HOLDING text is plain. Colour appears only for AOG and CAUTION / WARNING alerts (an inset bar) and NEEDS YOU (a blue bar on the alert band). The callsign is sans, mono is kept for IDs, codes, names and numbers, the card uses three text sizes and one uppercase heading level, numbers are tabular, and the card has one divider under the header plus 1 px rules between columns on wide cards. LOGBOOK is a small table (FLIGHT · PR · time · NET · date; no `ON TIME` text, a late time is amber); FUEL and PERFORMANCE are label / value rows with the target as a faint caption next to the value; ACCOUNT usage is a thin bar with `32%` and `resets`; the `TARGETS` line is gone because each target now sits beside its value. Buttons in the card are transparent with a 1 px border, and ATTACH and `⋯` carry inline icons. Only the card changed; the other FLEET panels look the same.
