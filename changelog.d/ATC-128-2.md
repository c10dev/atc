### Changed

- TOWER sends `GO AROUND` (W/U) to the holders of a PR that conflicts with base, falls behind it, or loses the PR ahead of it in the LANDING SEQUENCE, with `landingQueue[].goAround.text` unchanged, also in a `reset: true` pass. APPROACH INFO no longer covers `dirty` and `behind`. A team treats GO AROUND as an instruction: READBACK, rebase or merge `origin/main`, resolve, run the checks, push with `--force-with-lease`, or answer UNABLE when the two PRs disagree on behaviour (ATC-128).
