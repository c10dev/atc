### Changed
- DUTY's language rule (Korean or English only, never Japanese or Chinese, ATC-150) is stated at the top of the DUTY manual and at the head of the brief that DUTY gets every turn, so it survives a NEW SHIFT. The server now checks each DUTY text and counts lines that have kana or Chinese characters and no Hangul (code, URLs, paths and `BEGIN DATA` blocks are skipped). It only warns: the text still reaches the SUPERVISOR. METRICS → OPERATIONS shows lines checked and flagged per UTC day, and each flagged line writes one FLIGHT RECORDER line with the session id and time ([docs/duty.md](docs/duty.md)). Tier `flagged`.
