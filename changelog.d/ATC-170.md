### Added
- RADIO R1 (ATC-170, [docs/radio.md](docs/radio.md) "R1 as built"). The server merges the recorded radio traffic (CLEARANCEs with their READBACK, ROGER and UNABLE, FLIGHT PLANs and RECALLs, CREW CHANGEs, ARRIVED reports, MCC and RTS) into transmissions on the DELIVERY, TOWER, GROUND and COMPANY frequencies. A call stays `open` until its reply is recorded, and `overdueAt` follows the existing READBACK rules. `GET /api/radio?since=&freq=&limit=` returns them newest last (default the last 6 hours, capped at 2000), and the opt-in `/api/events` topic `radio` sends new or changed ones. Read only: no new state file, no record format change, no transcript read, and the default event set is unchanged.
